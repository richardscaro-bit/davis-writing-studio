Course AI doctrine

Level 2 · AI for Support, Not Composition

You are the writer. AI may support thinking; AI may not write your essay for you. This page explains Level 2 for the Case Study unit.

Allowed

Brainstorming questions, testing organization, reader-style feedback on work you already drafted, checking that your outline covers required moves.

Not allowed

Generating thesis statements, body paragraphs, paraphrases of sources you have not read, Works Cited entries you have not verified, or any final essay prose presented as your own.

If you use AI

Keep a short record: what you asked, what you kept or rejected, and how you verified claims yourself. Disclose when the assignment requires it.

Case Study unit note Critical analysis depends on your reading of the packet and 3–4 outside sources beyond the packet. Do not treat AI summaries as evidence.
