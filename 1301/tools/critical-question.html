Purpose

This page helps you shape one open, arguable critical question from your topic. Use it before you draft a thesis.

Use this page to

  • Carry the question you keep into the thesis generator
Practice only · you own the question

A strong critical question cannot be finished with a simple yes, no, or a single looked-up fact. Pick an essay above if you want matched starters.

Example only (made-up topic, not a class case)

Topic: a campus night market called Pecan Street Nights advertises itself as zero waste, while most stalls still use disposable trays.

  • Weaker, yes/no: Is Pecan Street Nights wasteful?
  • Weaker, fact-only: How many disposable trays does the market use on a Saturday?
  • Stronger: To what extent does the market's zero-waste message hold up once everyday stall practice is in view?
