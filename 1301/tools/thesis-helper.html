Fill in what you noticed in your readings or notes for the essay you are on. This tool builds 2 to 4 draft thesis options from structured templates (AI Level 2; no chatbot). Practice only · you own the claim

Optional early play is fine, but thesis work lands in Step 3 after demo + brainstorm. Avoid announcement theses (“This paper will…”). Purpose stays simple: one clear claim + packet evidence + 3–4 outside sources later.

Draft claim options

Copy one you like, edit it in your own voice, then Use this claim to carry it into the outline helper. These are templates — verify against your packet.

After you pick one — cue check
  • Is it arguable (a reader could disagree)?
  • Is it specific to this packet / case (not a vague opinion)?
  • Does it stay tied to the packet (naming the case + a real tension)?
  • Did you drop announcement openings (“This paper will…”)?
