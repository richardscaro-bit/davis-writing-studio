Practice tool · Week 14 · Case Study

Peer-review checklist

Rehearse feedback on a Case Study draft (about 1,000 to 1,500 words MLA, packet plus 3 to 4 outside sources, AI Level 2). Check what is working, leave a short note, then copy or print a summary for your partner.

Be specific · be kind · be honest

Point to a sentence or section when you can. Name strengths first. Flag AI Level 2 honesty without policing — ask where AI helped and whether the writer still owns the claim and evidence.
