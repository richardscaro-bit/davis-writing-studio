Practice helpers · any essay

Writing tools

Helpers for whichever essay you are on. Open a tool, choose the essay if that helper asks, then draft a practice critical question, thesis, topic sentences, body paragraph, research plan, keywords, citations, source notes, or peer feedback. Essay rooms live on Home under Essays.

Purpose

These tools stay generic until you pick an essay inside a helper. No tool assumes Case Study unless you select it.

How the Studio tools connect

Ready Critical question developer Turn a topic into an open, arguable question (how, why, to what extent), then send it to the thesis generator. Ready Thesis generator Pick an essay (or stay generic), fill the fields, get 2 to 4 draft theses to edit and carry into the outline. Ready MLA citation generator Practice Works Cited and in-text for web, article, journal, book, film, or course packet / handout. Verify every entry. Ready Outline / topic sentences Carry your thesis into an outline. Essay picker adapts section lenses; generic mode still works. Ready Body Paragraph Builder Draft one Case Study body paragraph in six moves, with sentence starters, a live preview, and a downloadable draft. Ready Source integration When to quote, paraphrase, or summarize; how to do each in MLA; a quote sandwich and a short practice with self-check. Ready Summaries, paraphrases, and quotes Practice a summary, a paraphrase, and a quote from one passage, then build a quote sandwich. Check for Strong, Medium, or Weak, and download in MLA 9. Ready Research Plan helper Plan your two library or Google Scholar sources: pick a topic family, try searches, think through ethics, and write a purpose line for each source. Ready Source log Track sources for any essay: notes, how each helps the thesis, MLA draft slots. Copy a summary anytime. Ready Peer-review checklist Rehearse feedback. Checklist items adapt when you pick an essay; generic list available by default. Ready Keyword generator Topic or thesis to keywords, synonyms, Boolean tips, Scholar and library query drafts. Local practice only. Ready CRAAP practice check Type answers for Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, and Purpose. Saved in this browser only.
