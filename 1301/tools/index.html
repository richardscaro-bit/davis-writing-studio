Practice helpers · any essay

Writing tools

Helpers for whichever essay you are on. Open a tool, choose the essay if that helper asks, then draft a practice thesis, topic sentences, keywords, citations, source notes, or peer feedback. Essay rooms live on Home under Essays.

Purpose

These tools stay generic until you pick an essay inside a helper. No tool assumes Case Study unless you select it.

Ready Thesis generator Pick an essay (or stay generic), fill the fields, get 2 to 4 draft theses to edit and carry into the outline. Ready MLA citation generator Practice Works Cited and in-text for web, article, journal, book, film, or course packet / handout. Verify every entry. Ready Outline / topic sentences Carry your thesis into an outline. Essay picker adapts section lenses; generic mode still works. Ready Source log Track sources for any essay: notes, how each helps the thesis, MLA draft slots. Copy a summary anytime. Ready Peer-review checklist Rehearse feedback. Checklist items adapt when you pick an essay; generic list available by default. Ready Keyword generator Topic or thesis to keywords, synonyms, Boolean tips, Scholar and library query drafts.
