Practice helpers · any essay

Writing tools

Helpers for whichever essay you are on. Open a tool to draft a practice thesis, draft topic sentences, build search keywords, practice citations, log sources, or rehearse peer feedback. Each page explains how to use that tool. Essay rooms live on Home under Essays.

Ready Thesis / claim generator Fill packet fields to get 2 to 4 draft theses you can copy, edit, and carry into the outline. Ready MLA citation generator Pick source type (web, article, journal, book, film, packet) to practice Works Cited and in-text. Verify every entry. Ready Outline / topic sentences Carry your thesis to draft topic sentences for framing, stakeholders, evidence, assumptions, limits, plus outside-source slots. Ready Source log Track packet plus 3 to 4 outside sources: notes, how each helps the thesis, MLA draft slots. Copy a summary anytime. Ready Peer-review checklist Rehearsal for thesis, packet plus outside sources, MLA, organization, AI Level 2 honesty: checkboxes plus notes. Ready Keyword generator Topic, thesis, or case to keywords, synonyms, Boolean tips, Scholar and library query drafts. Local practice only. 07 Essays on Home Open the essay room for the assignment you are practicing (Comparison, Exposition, Case Study, and more).
