Comparison · Monster Comparison

Monster Comparison Studio

A practice room for the Monster Comparison essay. Choose two iconic monsters that share meaningful ground, apply the same criteria to both, and draft a short MLA comparison that ends in one clear insight. This room is essay-first; no character cards required here.

Practice path Thesis helper Outline helper Peer-review checklist

Purpose

Compare two iconic monsters from the same meaningful category using consistent criteria and one insight beyond a list. Aim for a 500 to 750 word MLA comparison (AI Level 2: support, not composition).

Sample comparison essays

Read for structure and insight, not for copying. Notice shared ground, parallel criteria, and how the thesis goes past a simple list.

Comparison moves (Write What Matters style)

  • Shared ground: name the category both monsters belong to (not “both are monsters” alone).
  • Consistent criteria: apply the same 3 to 4 lenses to each figure.
  • Insight: say what the split reveals about culture, fear, power, or audience.
  • Audience: shape examples and tone for classmates or another named reader group.
