Purpose

One clear thesis that previews all five criteria in order, plus evidence from your Source Packet and Case Brief, plus exactly 2 outside sources. Write five body sections (framing, stakeholders, evidence quality, assumptions, limits). Each section uses two cited packet details. In exactly two sections, add one outside source each. Do not reuse a source. Aim for a 1,000 to 1,500 word MLA critical analysis (AI Level 2: support, not composition).

Research help

You need exactly 2 outside sources beyond your packet. The Case Study research guide walks through a search plan, SAC databases, Google Scholar, judging sources, the Source Log, and MLA.
