Cultural Place Field Guide Studio

A practice room for the face-to-face Field Guide project. Explain a place, practice, or local system for a named audience. Build one plate and a how/why essay that match each other. You explain; you do not take a side.

Practice path Plate labels Source log Outline helper Facing the Unknown

Purpose

Explain one focused how or why question about a place, practice, or local system for a named audience. Build one Field Guide plate plus a 750 to 1,000 word MLA essay with 1 to 3 credible sources (AI Level 2: support, not composition).

Plate labels (one page)

Fill every label before you draft the essay. The plate and the essay should describe the same place and audience.

  • Name
  • Where it is
  • Who it is for
  • How it works (3 to 5 notes)
  • What a newcomer notices first
  • One checkable source
  • One caution

Essay that matches the plate

  • One how or why question (if it has should, shrink it; that is argument).
  • Named audience.
  • 1 to 3 sources a reader can open.
  • 2 to 3 subtopics that match the plate.
  • 750 to 1,000 words MLA plus Works Cited.

How to use this studio

Practice path

1Name place and audienceOne line each; keep them stable through the draft. 2Write the how/whyCut any version that takes a side. 3Fill the plateAll seven labels before paragraph one of the essay. 4Log 1 to 3 sourcesEach source has a job that matches a subtopic. 5Draft from the plateDo not invent a new topic in paragraph two. 6ReviseCut side-taking, source dumping, and blank plate labels.
AI Level 2

AI may help list plate labels or give feedback after you have notes. The plate and the submitted sentences are yours.
