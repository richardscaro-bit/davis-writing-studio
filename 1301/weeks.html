Weeks 11–15 · Case Study

Here is a short map of what each Case Study week is for and where to practice. The path runs from an in-class demo and brainstorm, to locking a working thesis in Week 13, then a draft, one peer review, and a polished essay. Open a week card for studio and Tools links.

Open Case Study studio Practice tools Resources

Soft purpose

One clear claim plus evidence from your Source Packet / Case Brief plus 3 to 4 credible outside sources beyond the packet. Aim for a 1,000 to 1,500 word MLA critical analysis (AI Level 2: support, not composition).

Week 11 · Nov 2–8
Focus · topic + brainstorm

Topic selection + Case Study preview

What this week is for: see one case demo in class (BarkBox / Crumbl models), pick a topic, and brainstorm early ideas. Your case Source Packet and Case Brief are the required unit readings. Do not lock a thesis yet; start with idea generation. Practice in the Case Study studio topic picker and brainstorm workspace.

  • In-class focus: Critical Question Workshop
Topic picker Brainstorm in studio Packet pointers
Week 12 · Nov 9–15
Focus · packet notes

Critical analysis reading notes

What this week is for: active reading of your Source Packet / Case Brief for the problem, stakeholders, evidence, assumptions or biases, and limits. Keep notes you can reuse in the outline and draft. Practice reading notes in the studio; log sources in the Source log tool.

  • In-class focus: Evidence Quality
Reading notes in studio Source log Packet Drive
Week 13 · Nov 16–22
Focus · claim + outline

Outline + research plan

What this week is for: shape a 1 to 2 page outline and research plan with a working thesis, and identify 3 to 4 outside sources beyond the packet. This is when the thesis locks after the demo and brainstorm. Practice with the thesis, outline, and source-log tools, or draft the outline in the studio.

  • In-class focus: Outline / Thesis / Outside Source
Thesis / claim tool Outline helper Source log Outline in studio
Week 14 · Nov 23–29 · Thanksgiving-aware
Focus · draft + peer

Working draft + peer review

What this week is for: build a working draft toward full essay length, integrate or placeholder the 3 to 4 outside sources, and rehearse one peer review. Practice drafting in the studio; use the peer-review checklist and MLA practice tools to prepare feedback and citations.

Draft in studio Peer notes Peer-review checklist MLA practice
Week 15 · Nov 30–Dec 6
Focus · polished essay

Polished critical analysis essay

What this week is for: finish a 1,000 to 1,500 word MLA critical analysis (Works Cited not in the word count) with packet evidence primary and 3 to 4 outside sources beyond the packet. Use the final checklist in the Case Study studio, the MLA generator, and the AI Level 2 guide (support, not composition).

  • In-class focus: Limits / Ethics
Final checklist MLA generator AI Level 2

Week 16 Final Reflection lives on a separate path — this Weeks map covers Weeks 11–15 only.
