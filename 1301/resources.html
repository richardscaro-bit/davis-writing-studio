Purpose

One clear claim plus evidence from your Source Packet / Case Brief plus 3 to 4 credible outside sources beyond the packet. Aim for a 1,000 to 1,500 word MLA critical analysis (AI Level 2: support, not composition).

Library search tips

Prefer SAC Library databases for the 3 to 4 outside sources beyond the packet. Start with your case name plus one tension word, then narrow by peer-reviewed or full text. Sign in with ACES when prompted. Ask a librarian if a paywall appears; campus access often unlocks it.

  • Use the keyword generator first, then paste a draft query into the library search or a database (Academic Search Complete, JSTOR, and similar).
  • Try subject headings or Subjects filters once you find one good article.
  • Save citations carefully; verify every MLA entry against the source and Purdue OWL or your handbook.

Google Scholar search help

Scholar is useful for discovering scholarly articles and tracking citations. Pair it with SAC access: open Scholar, then Settings, Library links, and search Alamo or San Antonio College so Full-Text links appear. Prefer library PDFs over random upload sites.

  • Quote exact phrases: "subscription fatigue"
  • Combine concepts with AND / OR: BarkBox AND loyalty AND "customer retention"
  • Use the year slider and “Cited by” to find newer or foundational work.
  • If Scholar shows only an abstract, search the title in SAC databases or request help.

AI Level 2

Support, not composition. AI may help you brainstorm questions, check organization, or get reader-style feedback on work you already drafted. AI may not write your thesis, body paragraphs, source paraphrases, or final essay prose. Do not treat AI output as evidence.
