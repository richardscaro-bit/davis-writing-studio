Stage overview
  • Due: Sun Feb 14
  • What you make: Three quoted passages on your named detail with MLA in-text citations, and one named critical approach explained from one credible source.
  • Length: Three passages plus one lens explanation. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: MLA 9.
  • Course outcomes: 3, 4, 5
  • Support built in: Week 4 conference; one allowed question revision; pair check.
Required reading for this stage
  • Charles W. Chesnutt, "The Conjurer's Revenge," from The Conjure Woman: Project Gutenberg
  • Mary E. Wilkins Freeman, "The Wind in the Rose-Bush": Project Gutenberg
  • H. P. Lovecraft, "The Colour Out of Space": Project Gutenberg
  • MLA 9 help: MLA Style Center
How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 2: Evidence and Lens Note when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
