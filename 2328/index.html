Literature Inquiry

The seven stages

Stage 1 · due Sun Jan 31Literature Inquiry ProposalOne primary story from the early readings (Bierce through Toomer), one named detail, one how or why question, why it matters, and an AI use statement. Stage 2 · due Sun Feb 14Evidence and Lens NoteThree quoted passages on your named detail with MLA in-text citations, and one named critical approach explained from one credible source. Stage 3 · due Thu Feb 25Reality versus Illusion Close ReadingAnalyze how the narrative technique shapes your named detail and the reader's sense of what is real. Stage 4 · due Sun Mar 7Source Conversation MapYour second text (any course reading from a different period, movement, or form), three scholarly sources labeled Supports, Complicates, or Counters, one historical or cultural context source, and a working MLA Works Cited. Stage 5 · due Thu Mar 25Thematic Comparison DraftBuild from Stage 3: the comparison, context, your three scholarly sources, and one counter or limitation. Stage 6 · due Sun Apr 11Integration Revision Memo and Showcase PlanTie each planned revision to a named comment from your conference, the tutor, or peer review, plus one paragraph choosing your Showcase format. Stage 7 · due Thu Apr 22Final PacketYour revised comparative paper (MLA), a Process Record with three required sentences and an AI use statement, and a checklist pointing to evidence for all five outcomes.

Late April to May 14

After the Final Packet

  • Packet feedback: Professor Davis returns Final Packet feedback by Thu Apr 29.
  • Showcase: A piece built from your inquiry in the format you chose: a creative piece, a multimedia presentation, a public-facing essay or blog post, or your own choice, with a short rationale naming the texts and techniques it draws on. Creative and multimedia work lives here, not in the Final Packet.
  • Survey Retrospective: A low-stakes review of authors, periods, and forms with two attempts. It takes the place of a final exam.
  • Revision window: If your Final Packet is missing outcome evidence, a revision window closes Sun May 9. Check Canvas for how it works in your section.
  • Final exam period: The Showcase is shared in the final exam period or in a discussion board.
