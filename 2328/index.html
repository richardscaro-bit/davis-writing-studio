Essays

Essay 1 · studioReality versus illusionSynthesize your reading of the course's literary works into a coherent scholarly essay. Engage themes, narrative techniques, and character with a clear thesis; apply relevant theory; situate the text historically or culturally; revise with peer feedback. Essay 2 · studioThematic comparisonWrite a thematic comparison essay on 20th century American literature. Choose a theme (identity, alienation, the American Dream, or confrontation with norms) across works by two authors from the course list (Jackson, Matheson, Bradbury, Plath, O'Connor, or Capote). Final · studioFinal ProjectComplete the semester's final project as a refined final draft. Integrate proposal work, peer feedback, and course insights into a polished literary analysis with a clear thesis and scholarly documentation.
