Stage overview
  • Due: Thu Mar 25
  • What you make: Build from Stage 3: the comparison, context, your three scholarly sources, and one counter or limitation.
  • Length: About 1,800 to 2,500 words. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: MLA 9.
  • Course outcomes: 1 to 5
  • Support built in: Draft conference window (Professor Davis or a Writing Studio tutor); Tutor Session; peer review.
  • Tutor Session: this is the stage where you work through your draft with a Writing Studio tutor. You can also practice with the Studio Tutor.
Required reading for this stage
  • Ray Bradbury, "The Veldt" In copyright. Title only here; reach it through the SAC Library or Canvas.
  • Sylvia Plath, "Morning Song" and "The Applicant": "Morning Song," Poetry Foundation · "The Applicant," Poetry Foundation
  • MLA 9 help: MLA Style Center
How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 5: Thematic Comparison Draft when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
