Stage overview
  • Due: Sun Jan 31
  • What you make: One primary story from the early readings (Bierce through Toomer), one named detail, one how or why question, why it matters, and an AI use statement.
  • Length: 400 to 600 words. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: MLA 9.
  • Course outcomes: 1, 2
  • Support built in: Start Here path in Canvas; Week 2 check; early help if a stage is missing.
  • Question Lock shape: "How does [author] use [named detail or technique] in [story] to blur reality and illusion, and why does that matter for [idea, value, or reader]?"
Required reading for this stage
  • Ambrose Bierce, "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge": Project Gutenberg
  • Edgar Allan Poe, "The Tell-Tale Heart": Poe Museum
  • Henry James, The Turn of the Screw: Project Gutenberg
  • Edith Wharton, "Afterward" and "The Lady's Maid's Bell": "Afterward," Project Gutenberg · "The Lady's Maid's Bell," Project Gutenberg
  • MLA 9 help: MLA Style Center
How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 1: Literature Inquiry Proposal when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
