Required reading

Readings by stage

Stage 1: Literature Inquiry Proposal
  • Ambrose Bierce, "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge": Project Gutenberg
  • Edgar Allan Poe, "The Tell-Tale Heart": Poe Museum
  • Henry James, The Turn of the Screw: Project Gutenberg
  • Edith Wharton, "Afterward" and "The Lady's Maid's Bell": "Afterward," Project Gutenberg · "The Lady's Maid's Bell," Project Gutenberg
Stage 2: Evidence and Lens Note
  • Charles W. Chesnutt, "The Conjurer's Revenge," from The Conjure Woman: Project Gutenberg
  • Mary E. Wilkins Freeman, "The Wind in the Rose-Bush": Project Gutenberg
  • H. P. Lovecraft, "The Colour Out of Space": Project Gutenberg
Stage 3: Reality versus Illusion Close Reading
  • F. Scott Fitzgerald, "The Curious Case of Benjamin Button": USF Lit2Go
  • William Faulkner, "A Rose for Emily" (1930 text): Wikisource
  • Zora Neale Hurston, "Spunk": Wikisource
  • Jean Toomer, "Blood-Burning Moon," from Cane: Project Gutenberg
Stage 4: Source Conversation Map
  • Shirley Jackson, The Haunting of Hill House In copyright. Title only here; reach it through the SAC Library or Canvas.
Stage 5: Thematic Comparison Draft
  • Ray Bradbury, "The Veldt" In copyright. Title only here; reach it through the SAC Library or Canvas.
  • Sylvia Plath, "Morning Song" and "The Applicant": "Morning Song," Poetry Foundation · "The Applicant," Poetry Foundation
Stage 6: Integration Revision Memo and Showcase Plan
  • Flannery O'Connor, "A Good Man Is Hard to Find" In copyright. Title only here; reach it through the SAC Library or Canvas.
  • Richard Matheson, "The Man Who Made the World": Project Gutenberg
  • Optional: Truman Capote, "Miriam" Optional. Title only; SAC Library.
Stage 7: Final Packet
  • Edgar Allan Poe, "The Black Cat," in The Works of Edgar Allan Poe, Volume 2: Project Gutenberg
  • Edith Wharton, The Writing of Fiction: Project Gutenberg
  • Anne Rice, "On Writing" page: annerice.com Read as a companion to revision.
  • Optional library reading: Stephen King, "The Cat from Hell" and "What Is Writing" (from On Writing) Optional. Titles only; find them through the SAC Library.
