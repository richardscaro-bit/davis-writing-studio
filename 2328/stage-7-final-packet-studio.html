Stage overview
  • Due: Thu Apr 22
  • What you make: Your revised comparative paper (MLA), a Process Record with three required sentences and an AI use statement, and a checklist pointing to evidence for all five outcomes.
  • Length: Revised paper plus Process Record. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: MLA 9.
  • Course outcomes: All five
  • Support built in: Final Packet help sessions.
  • Process Record sentences: (1) your locked question and how it changed; (2) a source decision; (3) a synthesis or counter decision.
Required reading for this stage
  • Edgar Allan Poe, "The Black Cat," in The Works of Edgar Allan Poe, Volume 2: Project Gutenberg
  • Edith Wharton, The Writing of Fiction: Project Gutenberg
  • Anne Rice, "On Writing" page: annerice.com Read as a companion to revision.
  • Optional library reading: Stephen King, "The Cat from Hell" and "What Is Writing" (from On Writing) Optional. Titles only; find them through the SAC Library.
  • MLA 9 help: MLA Style Center
How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 7: Final Packet when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
