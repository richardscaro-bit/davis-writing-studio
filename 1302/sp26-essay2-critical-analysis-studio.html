Monster essays · Essay 2

Critical Analysis Studio

A practice room for the Critical Analysis essay. Stranger Things is required. Analyze a symbol, character, or theme, and bring in optional theory when it helps. Guides live on the Essays overview Doc; Tools links open practice helpers. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).

Practice path Practice tools Resources Monster essays

Purpose

Analyze one Stranger Things symbol, character, or theme with close attention to media, rhetoric, or literature. Support the thesis with secondary sources; optional theory may include Caruth, Herman, hooks, Sontag, or Cohen (AI Level 2: support, not composition).

Reading and skills cues

Readings. Primary: Stranger Things (required). Focus options include the Upside Down, Hawkins Lab, Will, Eleven, Vecna, and 1980s nostalgia. Optional companion: a graphic novel or webcomic for visual rhetoric (see Approaching Graphic Novels). Optional theory: Caruth, Herman, hooks, Sontag, Cohen.

Skills. Media, rhetorical, or literary analysis plus secondary sources.

Assignment materials

Course Text Section II and graphic novels guide

Student facing guides, case studies, samples, and the rubric for Essay 2 live in the 1302 Course Text (Section II). Use Approaching Graphic Novels when you work a visual or graphic companion. The Essays overview Doc holds the sequence overview.
