Analyze how one public-facing text works on readers, then remix that argument into a new mode, genre, audience, or circulation setting. Submit a multimodal artifact plus a companion essay (~1,000 words MLA).

Assignment overview
  • Analyze one public-facing text (exigence, purpose, audience, genre, setting), then remix into a new mode, genre, audience, or circulation setting.
  • Companion essay ~1,000 words MLA (usable range 900–1,100). Artifact: infographic/poster, social carousel, 2–4 min audio/video with transcript, annotated redesign, short web page/one-pager, or approved form.
  • Process: proposal + working thesis; draft workshop; revision memo; final. AI Level 2.
Required reading / materials
  • Primary: one public-facing text you choose (ad, PSA, editorial, campaign page, campus/city notice, nonprofit appeal, short documentary clip, or similar). Must be citable and shareable.
  • Course OER alignment: Composition for Commodores — rhetorical situation, rhetorical analysis, revision (Prepare → Attend → Practice → Submit).
  • No fixed literary packet beyond your chosen public text.
