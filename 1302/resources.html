Resources

Here is a short map of practice support for Composition II: Fall project pack pointers, Spring 2026 past term essay studios, research help, and links into each practice room. Open a card to see what that resource is for.

Purpose

Use these materials to practice the project or past term essay you are on. Each studio restates Purpose and links its pack or design Doc.

Project 1Rhetorical Analysis and RemixAnalyze how an existing text works on readers, then remix it for a new purpose and audience. Pair a multimodal remix with about a 1,000 word MLA rhetorical analysis and reflection (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Project 2Source Conversation ExhibitBuild a source conversation exhibit of four to six research sources plus a 900 to 1,100 word MLA companion. Show where sources align, vary, contradict, and stop. Name the research question, audience, each source role, and set boundaries (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Project 3Gothic Literary CriticismWrite a 1,500 to 2,000 word MLA literary criticism essay on one Gothic primary text. State a contestable interpretive thesis, support it with close reading, and engage at least two peer-reviewed critics from two different criticism types (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Project 4Public-Facing Researched ArgumentCreate a multimodal researched argument for a real public audience, then write a 1,000 word research and design reflection. Use at least seven sources (three scholarly), a clear thesis, counterargument, and documented audience testing (AI Level 2: support, not composition).
