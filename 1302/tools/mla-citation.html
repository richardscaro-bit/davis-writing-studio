Quick cue check
  • Author in Works Cited = last name first; in-text uses last name only (or short title if no author).
  • Titles of short works (articles, pages, episodes) use quotation marks; books, journals, films, sites are italicized.
  • If your essay uses a course packet or handout, cite it once, then each outside source.
  • Hanging indent in your real doc: first line flush left; following lines indented 0.5".
