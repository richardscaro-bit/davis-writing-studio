Essays

Eight essays in one list. Open the studio for the essay you are practicing.

Essay 1 · studio Rhetorical Analysis and Remix Analyze how an existing text works on readers, then remix it for a new purpose and audience. Pair a multimodal remix with about a 1,000 word MLA rhetorical analysis and reflection (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Essay 2 · studio Source Conversation Exhibit Build a source conversation exhibit of four to six research sources plus a 900 to 1,100 word MLA companion. Show where sources align, vary, contradict, and stop. Name the research question, audience, each source role, and set boundaries (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Essay 3 · studio Gothic Literary Criticism Write a 1,500 to 2,000 word MLA literary criticism essay on one Gothic primary text. State a contestable interpretive thesis, support it with close reading, and engage at least two peer-reviewed critics from two different criticism types (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Essay 4 · studio Public-Facing Researched Argument Create a multimodal researched argument for a real public audience, then write a 1,000 word research and design reflection. Use at least seven sources (three scholarly), a clear thesis, counterargument, and documented audience testing (AI Level 2: support, not composition). Essay 5 · studio Evaluation Essay Evaluate a cultural or literary monster through clear criteria. Skills: criteria thesis, judgment, intro secondary research. No Stranger Things. Essay 6 · studio Critical Analysis (Stranger Things) Analyze a Stranger Things symbol, character, or theme. Skills: media, rhetorical, or literary analysis plus secondary sources. Stranger Things required. Essay 7 · studio Argument Essay Claim who or what the real monster is (literary, cultural, or social). Skills: argument, research, counterargument. Stranger Things optional reference only. Essay 8 · studio Final Research Essay Formal synthesis and evaluation on culture, fear, monstrosity, or community (not a zine). Skills: synthesis, evaluation, MLA 9. Five to seven sources.
