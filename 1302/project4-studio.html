Assignment overview
  • Multimodal researched argument for a real public audience and circulation setting, plus a ~1,000-word research-and-design reflection.
  • At least seven sources including at least three scholarly sources in differentiated roles; clear qualified claim; counterargument; documented audience/usability test and revision; accessible alternatives and media credits.
  • Note: Confirm with your instructor whether Project 4 is active in your live section (some Fall notes describe a three-project sequence plus a short final reflection).
Required reading / materials
  • No fixed primary literary list. Student research set (≥7 sources, ≥3 scholarly).
  • Process supports: research question/proposal work; annotated bibliography practice.
