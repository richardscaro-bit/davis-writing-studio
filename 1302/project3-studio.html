Gothic Literary Criticism Studio

A practice room for Essay 3. Choose a primary Gothic text, locate two critics of different types, outline the interpretive thesis, then draft the literary criticism essay.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All essays

Purpose

Write a 1,500 to 2,000 word MLA literary criticism essay on one Gothic primary text. State a contestable interpretive thesis, support it with close reading, and engage at least two peer-reviewed critics from two different criticism types (AI Level 2: support, not composition).
