Assignment overview
  • Build a multimodal exhibit of 4–6 research sources (at least two categories) showing consensus, variation, contradiction, scope, and boundaries.
  • Companion essay ~1,000 words MLA: research question, audience, source roles, boundaries. Pair two sources in every body paragraph; one properly introduced quotation per body paragraph.
Required reading / materials
  • Named course reading: Critical Thinking in Academic Research (Part VII) — primary / secondary / tertiary source categories.
  • Student-chosen research set: 4–6 sources for your question (peer-reviewed, institutional/gov, datasets/methodology, professional trade).
