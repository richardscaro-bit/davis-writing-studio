A practice room for Essay 2. Shape the research question and audience, gather four to six sources, build the exhibit, then draft the companion essay.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All essays

Purpose

Build a source conversation exhibit of four to six research sources plus a 900 to 1,100 word MLA companion. Show where sources align, vary, contradict, and stop. Name the research question, audience, each source role, and set boundaries (AI Level 2: support, not composition).
