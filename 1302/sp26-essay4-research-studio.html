ENGL 1302 · Essay 8

Final Research Essay Studio

A practice room for the Final Research Essay. This is formal synthesis and evaluation, not a zine. Evaluate and synthesize scholarship on culture, fear, monstrosity, or community. Guides live on the Essays overview Doc; Tools links open practice helpers. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).

Practice path Practice tools Resources All essays

Purpose

Evaluate and synthesize research on an issue connected to monsters, fear, or community. Aim for 5–7 sources including scholarly and cultural/literary texts (MLA 9). Aim for a maximum of 1,000 words.

Reading and skills cues

Readings. Build a focused source set of five to seven items: at least one scholarly source and at least one cultural or literary source. Stranger Things is optional as one case among others, not the whole essay.

Skills. Synthesis, evaluation, and MLA 9.

Assignment overview
  • Evaluate and synthesize research on a cultural, social, or psychological issue connected to monsters, fear, or community.
  • Source bar: at least 5–7 credible sources; ≥1 scholarly article; ≥1 cultural/literary text; MLA 9. Aim for a maximum of 1,000 words.
Required reading / materials
  • Student-chosen research set meeting the source bar.
  • Topics may include community fear, cultural significance of monsters, trauma in media, institutional panics, nostalgia/identity, folklore and trauma.
  • Stranger Things may be one case study, not the center (unless your instructor allows).
