### 1. Project 1 — Rhetorical Analysis and Remix

1. **Name:** Rhetorical Analysis and Remix  
2. **Short purpose:** Analyze how one public-facing text works on readers (exigence, purpose, audience, genre, setting), then remix that argument into a new mode, genre, audience, or circulation setting. Submit a multimodal artifact plus a companion essay.  
3. **Length / criteria gist (pasteable):**

### 2. Project 2 — Source Conversation Exhibit

1. **Name:** Source Conversation Exhibit
