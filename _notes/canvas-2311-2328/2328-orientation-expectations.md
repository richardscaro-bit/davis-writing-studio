Course Expectations




 Evaluation of this course is based on the following assignments:

    Daily Assignments: 15%
    Major Essay #1: 15%
    Major Essay #2: 15%
    Major Essay # 3: 15%
    Final Project and Exam: 40%

 *The Final Project will be used as a Formative Assessment for the Learning Outcomes.

  Breakdown
 This syllabus breakdown outlines how your performance in the course will be assessed. The course is
 designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of American horror literature, focusing on various
 themes, authors, and literary techniques. Your participation, essays, and final project and exam will
 contribute to your overall grade as follows:

  1. Daily Assignments - 15%
        These include short responses to readings, participation in discussion forums, and other small
        tasks assigned weekly.
        The purpose of these assignments is to ensure ongoing engagement with the course material and
        to facilitate regular contributions to class discussions.
       Daily assignments will be evaluated based on completion, thoughtfulness, and engagement with
       the course materials.
 2. Major Essay #1 - 15%
       The first major essay will focus on the themes and narrative techniques in one or two of the works
       studied in the first part of the course.
       This essay will be a critical analysis, approximately 1,500-2,000 words in length.
       Assessment criteria will include clarity of argument, depth of analysis, engagement with the
       text(s), and writing quality.
 3. Major Essay #2 - 15%
       The second major essay will build upon the themes explored in the first essay, allowing for a
       deeper and more comprehensive analysis of the chosen texts.
       Similar in length to the first essay, this assignment will further assess your ability to analyze
       literature and articulate your insights critically.
       Grading will focus on the complexity of the argument, the integration of critical perspectives, and
       the overall cohesiveness of the essay.
 4. Major Essay #3 - 15%
       The third major essay will provide an opportunity to explore a specific theme, author, or literary
       technique in depth.
       This essay should demonstrate your ability to synthesize and critique multiple works or a broader
       literary concept within the genre of American horror literature.
       Evaluation will be based on originality of thought, analytical depth, and effective communication of
       ideas.
 5. Final Project and Exam - 40%
       The final project will be a culmination of your learning throughout the course. It can be a research
       paper, creative project, or multimedia presentation.
       The final exam will test your comprehensive understanding of the course material, including key
       themes, authors, and literary concepts.
       The final project and exam will be assessed for depth of understanding, creativity (where
       applicable), and the ability to synthesize and apply course concepts.

General Guidelines:

   Adherence to deadlines is crucial. Late submissions may result in grade deductions unless prior
   arrangements are made.
   Participation in discussions and activities is critical. Engage regularly and constructively with your
   classmates and course materials.
   Academic integrity is paramount. Ensure that all work is your own and that sources are properly cited.

This course is designed to be both challenging and rewarding, allowing you to immerse yourself in the
rich and diverse world of American horror literature. We look forward to your contributions and intellectual
growth throughout this journey!
