Job Application

 Objective
   1. To develop essential job application skills by creating a resume cover letter, researching employer
      expectations, and establishing a professional brand on LinkedIn, and presenting these materials on a
      personal portfolio website or a platform of the student's choice.


 Tasks
You complete each part of this assignment and upload it on your portfolio or website.

VIDEO on ASSIGNMENT ---> See the 3rd part in the reading

Part 1: Resume Writing

   Task: Create a comprehensive, professional resume.

   Focus: Emphasize technical writing skills, education, and relevant experiences.

Part 2: Cover Letter Creation

   Task: Write a tailored cover letter for a hypothetical job in technical writing.

   Focus: Illustrate alignment of your skills and experiences with job requirements.

   Review: Obtain feedback from a friend or acquaintance and make revisions based on input.

Part 3: Researching Employer Expectations

   Research Assignment: Investigate employer preferences for technical writing candidates.

       Method: Conduct interviews, online research on LinkedIn or employer websites, or surveys with HR
       professionals.

Part 4: Establishing a Professional Brand on LinkedIn

   LinkedIn Profile Creation:

       Task: Develop or enhance your LinkedIn profile, showcasing your technical writing expertise and
       professional history.

       Elements: Include a professional photo, engaging summary, detailed experience section, and listed skills.

   LinkedIn Networking and Branding:

       Task: Formulate a LinkedIn networking and branding approach.
      Activities: Participate in industry groups, follow relevant organizations, post industry-related content, and
      network with professionals.

Final Submission on Portfolio Website

   Compilation: Assemble all components into a comprehensive job application package or page.

   Components: Include your polished resume, customized cover letter, summary of research on employer
   expectations, and a link to your LinkedIn profile.

   Platform: Submit the entire package on your personal portfolio website or a platform of your choice.

See the Final Portfolio assignment on developing your portfolio.
