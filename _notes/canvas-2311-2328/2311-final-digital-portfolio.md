Technical Writing Portfolio Assignment: Compilation &
Progress Report
For your final, you will create a website or an e-portfolio of the work that you have completed in this class. You will
utilize all of the elements you have learned to create your brand and portfolio for your future work.

Objectives
   Recognize, analyze, and accommodate diverse audiences: Your portfolio will demonstrate your skill in
   crafting communication tailored to varied audiences.
   Produce documents appropriate to audience, purpose, and genre: You'll select works that show your
   versatility across different contexts and purposes.
   Analyze the ethical responsibilities involved in technical communication: Your work will reflect an
   understanding of ethical standards in the information you present and how you present it.
   Locate, evaluate, and incorporate pertinent information: Evidence of thorough research and the ability to
   discern and integrate relevant information will be critical.
   Develop verbal, visual, and multimedia materials as necessary: Your portfolio will include a range of
   formats, showcasing your adaptability and proficiency in various mediums.
   Edit for appropriate style: The documents within your portfolio will be meticulously revised for clarity, style,
   and grammatical precision.
   Design and test documents for easy reading and navigation: The organization and design of your portfolio
   itself will demonstrate your ability to create user-friendly documents.
