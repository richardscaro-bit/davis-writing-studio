 The culmination of our initial explorations and discussions in this course, Major Essay #1, is an opportunity
 to synthesize your understanding of the literary works we've studied, incorporating feedback from the peer
 review process to refine and deepen your analysis. This essay will demonstrate your ability to critically
 engage with texts, apply theoretical concepts, and articulate your insights in a coherent and scholarly
 manner.




  Objectives
  1. Critical Engagement: Demonstrate a deep and nuanced understanding of the selected literary work(s),
     engaging critically with themes, narrative techniques, and character development.
  2. Theoretical Application: Apply relevant literary theories and concepts to analyze the text(s), showing
     how these frameworks illuminate your understanding of the literature.
  3. Contextual Analysis: Situate the text(s) within their historical, cultural, or social contexts, explaining
     how these contexts influence the interpretation of the work.
  4. Scholarly Communication: Write a well-organized, clear, and argumentatively compelling essay,
    utilizing scholarly conventions of citation and documentation to support your analysis.
