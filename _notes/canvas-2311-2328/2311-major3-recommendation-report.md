Major Assignment # 3: Your Recommendation Report
Technical writing is more than just documenting facts or presenting information. It's about influencing decisions,
driving change, and proposing solutions to complex issues. In the professional world, a well-researched and
articulated recommendation report can be the difference between adopting a transformative initiative and a missed
opportunity. The essence of this assignment lies not just in presenting information but in the meticulous analysis,
synthesis, and recommendation based on that information. Building on your preliminary proposal, you will delve
deeper into the problem or opportunity you've identified, rigorously evaluate potential solutions, and ultimately
champion a specific course of action.


 Objectives
   Produce documents tailored to audience, purpose, and genre: Craft a recommendation report that
   effectively communicates a well-reasoned course of action based on thorough analysis.
   Analyze diverse audiences and accommodate their perspectives: Consider various stakeholders in the
   problem or opportunity and how different solutions might affect them.
   Integrate ethical responsibilities in technical communication: Ensure that all claims and conclusions are
   well-founded and presented with integrity.
   Employ advanced research skills to locate, evaluate, and incorporate pertinent information: Build upon
   the research from the proposal, integrating new insights and sources to present a holistic view of the problem or
   opportunity.
   Design documents for easy reading and navigation: Structure the report logically and coherently, ensuring
   clarity and ease of understanding.
   Revise and edit for clarity, coherence, and consistency in style and tone: Reflect on feedback from the
   proposal stage and polish the report to meet professional standards.


 Assignment
Building upon your previously submitted Proposal and Progress report, you will now construct a detailed
Recommendation Report. This report should identify a specific problem or opportunity, evaluate potential solutions
or courses of action, and culminate in a well-founded recommendation

Structure and Content:
   1. Introduction:
         Problem or Opportunity Identification: Begin by detailing the specific problem or opportunity from your
         Proposal. Why is it significant? How has your understanding of it evolved since your initial proposal?
         Criteria Establishment: Clearly outline the criteria you will use to evaluate potential solutions or
         responses, drawing upon the parameters you set in your Proposal.

    2. Methods:

         Option Determination: List the various options you're considering. Reflect upon the methods and
         sources used in your Proposal to identify these options, and discuss any new methods you employed.

    3. Results:

         Option Evaluation: Present a detailed examination of each option against your established criteria.
         Ensure each option undergoes rigorous evaluation, highlighting areas previously mentioned in your
         Proposal.

    4. Conclusions:

         Analysis Synthesis: Derive conclusions about each option's efficacy, feasibility, and potential impact.
         Reflect on any changes or refinements from your Proposal's preliminary conclusions.

    5. Recommendations:

         Formulation of Suggestions: Recommend a course of action based on your conclusions. Relate back to
         your Proposal to discuss how these recommendations might differ or align with your initial thoughts.


Criteria
    Integrate the Proposal: Consistently refer back to your Proposal assignment, highlighting where your research
    and insights have expanded or shifted.

    Research Depth: Your report should be substantiated by robust research. Draw from sources introduced in
    your Proposal and integrate new findings to enhance credibility.

    Objectivity: Maintain an impartial perspective. Your conclusions should be guided by data and results, not
    personal biases.

    Clarity & Precision: Articulate your findings clearly and precisely. Clarify any technical or specialized terms
    used.

    Formatting: Keep consistent formatting throughout your report, mirroring the professional presentation of your
    Proposal.

    Originality: While your Proposal serves as a starting point, ensure your conclusions and recommendations in
    this report are in-depth and tailored to the problem or opportunity at hand.
