 Your second major essay is to engage deeply with the thematic landscapes of 20th-century American
 literature. By examining the works of Shirley Jackson, Richard Matheson, Ray Bradbury, Sylvia Plath,
 Flannery O'Connor, or Truman Capote, you will explore a central theme of your choice—such as identity,
 alienation, the American Dream, or the confrontation with societal norms. This essay aims to illuminate how
 these authors articulate and examine human experiences within their unique cultural and historical contexts.




  Objectives
  1. Thematic Analysis: Identify and analyze a central theme that connects works by two of the authors
     listed. Your essay should demonstrate how this theme is developed and explored through the authors'
     distinctive narrative techniques and settings.
  2. Contextual Understanding: Examine the historical and cultural backdrops of each selected work to
     understand better how they influence the portrayal and interpretation of your chosen theme.
  3. Comparative Insight: Offer a comparative synthesis that highlights the similarities and differences in
     how the authors approach the theme. Discuss the broader significance of this theme in the context of
     American literature and culture.
  4. Reflective Engagement: Reflect on the contemporary relevance of the theme, considering its
     implications for today's societal issues and its impact on your understanding of society and identity
