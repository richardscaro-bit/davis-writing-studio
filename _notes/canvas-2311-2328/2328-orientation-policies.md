Course Policies




                           Attendance and Participation
 Success in this course depends on active participation and consistent engagement with assignments,
 discussions, and peer interactions. Students must complete all work by the deadlines and contribute to
 class discussions thoughtfully.

    Assignments are scheduled throughout the week, with essays and assignments typically due on
    Sundays.
    Contact me immediately if you need additional time due to work, family, or personal commitments. It
    is difficult to catch up once you fall behind.
    Participation in peer reviews and discussions means more than just posting once. It requires
    meaningful engagement with classmates and the instructor.
    Professionalism and respect are expected in all interactions.

   Carefully review and read materials that you receive electronically to ensure that you fully understand
   the message;
   Be sure to carefully re-read and understand what you will be sending in order to ensure that you are
   not misunderstood by anyone;

   If you are responding to a message, either include the relevant part of the original message in your

   If your messages can be typed in UPPER and lower case, please use the two appropriately instead
   of all UPPERCASE characters. This gives the appearance of shouting and makes the message less
   readable;
   Remember that not all readers have English as their native language, so make allowance for possible
   misunderstandings and unintended discourtesies;
   Do not abuse new users of computer networks for their lack of knowledge;
   Follow the same standards of politeness as you do in any other aspect of your life.

                       Student Success Optional Resources
As a marketable skill for communication and San Antonio College's strategic priority: student success,
student writers can better hone their writing skills when they dedicate time to the writing process and
have the opportunity to discuss their writing with others.

Therefore, this course includes an optional student success opportunity for our essays. The student
success opportunity can be met in one of two ways for each assignment, and you do not have to choose
the same option for each.

Option 1: Meet with a Writing Tutor in the English Success Center

Make an appointment to meet with a writing tutor in the English Success Center to review and discuss
your rough draft before submitting your final draft. Tutoring appointments should take place at least two
days before the paper’s final due date for you to allow enough time to revise your paper based on the
work you accomplish together. Meeting with a tutor from the Writing Center is not punitive; writers should
be open to feedback from other writers in the process of improving the effectiveness of their messages.
Take your tutoring appointment seriously; you should be adequately prepared with enough material to
gain the most from your time with the tutor. If your tutor does not feel you are prepared for or engaged
during your appointment, he or she may decide NOT to issue you the Proof of Visit form required for you
to obtain the points allocated to the student success requirement.

Option 2: Attend an English Success Center Workshop Session

Instead of meeting one-on-one with a writing tutor, you may elect to attend a Workshop to meet your
student success opportunity. If you select this option, you must attend one workshop related to that
assignment. (See the Writing Center section below for more information on workshops this summer).
SAC English Success Center

English Success Center Workshops will be available during the semester. Please see their website for
when the workshops will be available. They are located in Gonzales Hall 118. The Center also provides
individual tutoring appointments. These are either by appointment or walk-in. If you choose to make an
appointment, see the Resource Module in Canvas on how to make an appointment. During your tutoring
sessions, please be prepared with your documents, the prompt, and any other required documents. You
must stay 30 minutes for the appointment to receive a signed “proof of visit” sheet.

You may attend either of the workshops or make an appointment during the semester to receive extra
credit. In exchange, you will receive ten bonus points for each session you attend that will be tacked on
to your points total at the end of the term. If the days/times of the Writing Center do not fit your class
schedule, alternatives are available to you. Please visit me for assistance.

                                       Academic Integrity

   Academic honesty is expected of students enrolled in this course. Cheating on examinations,
   unauthorized collaboration, falsification of research data, plagiarism, and undocumented use of
   materials from any source, constitutes academic dishonesty. Additional work created by any
   AIChat or online AI software without express consent from the instructor can also be considered
   academically dishonest. Work will be checked for plagiarism using Turnitin and GPTZero. Any
   submitted assignments discovered to have violated academic integrity will be grounds for a grade
   of "F" in the course and/or further disciplinary actions. For additional information, see the college
   catalog.

   Additional AI Policy

   In our course, we may incorporate various Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies and tools to
   enhance learning experiences and support academic endeavors. Responsible and ethical use of
   these tools is paramount.

   AI Usage Limitation

       AI-assisted content in each essay should not exceed 15% of the total content. This ensures
       the primary focus remains on the student's original ideas and writing skills. The AI assistance
       includes Grammarly, Translators, and any Word-Processing program that uses AI-generated
       content.

   Guidelines for AI Use

    1. Purpose of AI Use: Utilize AI for initial brainstorming, generating outlines, or suggestions for
       grammar, style, and structure improvements.
    2. Originality and Reworking: Students must significantly modify AI-generated content to ensure
       their final submission primarily reflects their own work.
 3. Mandatory Disclosure: All AI usage must be explicitly indicated at the end of the essay in the
    works cite page with a statement detailing the extent and nature of AI assistance.
 4. Ethical Considerations: AI should be used ethically, adhering to academic integrity principles.
    Avoid using AI for deceptive purposes or plagiarism.
 5. Research and Citation: Independently verify AI-assisted research information and cite all
   sources, including AI contributions.

Addressing Misrepresentation and Non-Use of AI

   Misrepresented AI Use: Instances where AI use is misrepresented (either as being more or
   less than the actual usage) will be taken seriously. This includes both overstating AI's role to
   diminish one's own effort and understating AI's contribution to suggest greater personal input.
   Non-Use of AI: In cases where students choose not to use AI tools, the authenticity of their
   work must be maintained. Any claims of non-use of AI will be subject to standard plagiarism
   checks to ensure the integrity of the work.
   Verification Measures: The course may employ various tools and methods to verify the extent
   of AI usage in submissions. These checks aim to identify both overreliance on AI and
   instances in which AI use is not properly disclosed.

Consequences of Policy Violation

   Non-compliance with this policy will be viewed as a breach of academic integrity.
   Consequences range from grade reductions (at my discretion, typically a 50 or below,
   depending on percentage usage) to more severe academic sanctions, based on the violation's
   nature and severity.

Encouraging Responsible Use

   This policy aims to encourage critical engagement with AI tools while upholding academic
   integrity.
   It promotes an educational environment where technology supplements learning without
   undermining individual effort and skill.

Policy Review and Feedback

   Students are encouraged to provide feedback on their AI usage experiences.
   The policy will be reviewed periodically for potential adjustments to align with the course's
   educational goals.

Academic Integrity Addendum:

All work you submit for this course must be original work completed for this course during this
semester. If you are repeating this class, you must construct new and original work for your
assignments, even if the assignments are similar or the same as previous semesters. Submitting
work you’ve completed previously is unethical and unfair to other students in the course.
