Introduction to Evaluation as a Rhetorical Mode
________________


What Is Evaluation?
Evaluation is a form of academic writing in which a writer makes a judgment about a subject by applying clear, fair, and defensible criteria.
Unlike personal opinions—“I liked it,” “It was boring,” or “That monster wasn’t scary”—evaluation requires reasoning, standards, and evidence.
Evaluation asks you to answer:
1. What standards should be used to measure the value or effectiveness of the subject?

2. How well does the subject meet or fail those standards?

3. What evidence supports this final judgment?

In other words, evaluation is judgment with justification.
________________


Why Evaluation Matters in ENGL 1302
In this course, you will evaluate:
   * Monsters (folkloric, literary, or pop culture)

   * Cultural artifacts (myths, rituals, horror tropes)

   * Literary works (short stories, symbolic narratives)

   * Symbolic figures (characters, archetypes, metaphors)

Through evaluation, you explore how and why these texts shape:
      * Social fears

      * Cultural identities

      * Moral debates

      * Community memory

      * Historical anxieties

Evaluation helps you move beyond “What happened?” and into the deeper question of:
“What does it mean, and how effectively is that meaning conveyed?”
________________


Evaluation vs. Opinion
Many students initially confuse these two, so let’s break it down clearly.
Opinion
A personal feeling, preference, or reaction.
         * “I don’t like Frankenstein’s monster.”

         * “La Llorona is scary.”

         * “This story was confusing.”

Opinion is not enough for academic writing.
________________


Evaluation
A judgment based on criteria, supported with evidence.
            * “Frankenstein’s Creature is an effective symbol of scientific responsibility because….”

            * “La Llorona remains culturally powerful due to her associations with grief, maternal fear, and colonial trauma.”

            * “The story is ineffective as a critique because….”

Evaluation uses:
✔ Analysis
✔ Standards
✔ Evidence
✔ Critical thinking
________________


Components of a Strong Evaluation
1. The Subject
The thing you are evaluating
(e.g., a monster, a text, a character, a cultural story)
2. Criteria
The standards you will use to judge it
(e.g., symbolic depth, cultural relevance, psychological impact)
3. Judgment
Your overall conclusion
(e.g., effective, ineffective, complex, harmful, powerful, incomplete)
4. Evidence
Quotes, examples, scenes, descriptions, research findings
5. Reasoning
How and why the evidence proves your judgment
________________


Example Breakdown
Let’s look at how evaluation works with a familiar monster:
SUBJECT: La Llorona
CRITERIA:
               * Cultural longevity

               * Psychological impact

               * Symbolic meaning

JUDGMENT:
La Llorona is a culturally enduring and effective folkloric monster because she symbolizes generational grief and the consequences of crossing moral or cultural boundaries.
EVIDENCE:
                  * Stories passed down through families for generations

                  * Regional variations across Latin America

                  * Themes of loss, motherhood, and mourning

REASONING:
Because her story reflects deep emotional fears—fear of losing children, fear of breaking social norms—La Llorona continues to function as a powerful moral and cultural warning.
This is evaluation—not “she’s scary” or “she’s interesting,” but a defensible claim supported with reasoning.
________________


Why Do We Use Evaluation in Humanities Writing?
Evaluation helps us understand:
✔ How cultural narratives shape public beliefs
Monsters often reflect fears about immigration, gender roles, technology, race, community safety, etc.
✔ Why certain stories survive longer than others
Enduring monsters often speak to unresolved cultural anxieties.
✔ How symbolic figures communicate values
Characters can represent capitalism, innocence, trauma, oppression, or hope.
✔ How genre works
Horror, folklore, and literary fiction all use different strategies to produce meaning.

Common Misunderstandings Students Have
Students often think evaluation means:
                              * “Tell me your feelings.” → Incorrect

                              * “Explain the plot or describe the monster.” → Incorrect

                              * “Say whether it’s good or bad.” → Incomplete

Instead, evaluation means:
🔥
Make a judgment.
Use criteria.
Support with evidence.
Explain your reasoning.


The Purpose of an Evaluation Essay
The purpose of an evaluation essay is to make a reasoned and defensible judgment about a subject by applying clear, fair, and appropriate standards known as criteria. Unlike informal opinion writing, an evaluation essay requires you to carefully determine:
                                 * what standards should be used to measure effectiveness or value,

                                 * how well the subject meets or fails those standards, and

                                 * what evidence proves your final judgment.

In ENGL 1302, evaluation essays help you develop critical thinking, strengthen analytical reading, and enhance your ability to interpret cultural and literary texts within broader social contexts. By evaluating monsters, folklore, literature, and symbolic figures, you learn to articulate how cultural narratives influence belief systems, fears, and community identities.
________________


Key Features of an Evaluation Essay
An effective evaluation essay includes the following components:
1. A Clear Subject
 This is the object, text, character, or cultural figure you are evaluating.
Examples: La Llorona, Frankenstein’s Creature, Pennywise, “The Lottery,” El Cucuy.
2. Defensible Criteria
 Criteria are the standards by which you judge the subject. They must be meaningful, relevant, and based on qualities that can be analyzed.
Examples: cultural relevance, symbolic meaning, psychological impact, originality, narrative function.
3. A Judgment-Based Thesis
 This thesis states your overall assessment of the subject and previews the criteria that shape your argument.
Example:
“La Llorona is an effective and culturally significant monster because she expresses generational trauma, embodies moral warning, and reflects historical grief.”
4. Evidence
 Your evaluation must be supported by examples, quotations, stories, research, or descriptive analysis.
5. Reasoning
 This is your explanation of how the evidence supports your judgment. Reasoning transforms summary into analysis.
________________


What an Evaluation Essay Is Not
Students often mistake evaluation for other types of writing. An evaluation essay is not:
                                    * a summary of the text or plot,

                                    * a personal reaction without support,

                                    * a description of the story or character,

                                    * a list of strengths and weaknesses with no criteria, or

                                    * a general commentary on whether something is “good” or “bad.”

Evaluation requires you to move beyond description and into interpretive analysis supported with standards and evidence.
________________


Structure of an Evaluation Essay
The evaluation essay follows a structured format that ensures clarity and organization. Below is the standard structure used in ENGL 1302.
Introduction
The introduction should:
                                       * identify and briefly describe the subject,

                                       * establish the purpose of your evaluation,

                                       * introduce the criteria you will use, and

                                       * end with a judgment-based thesis.

Example Introduction
 Folkloric monsters often reflect the fears and values of the communities that created them. One enduring figure in Mexican and Tejano cultural storytelling is La Llorona, a ghostly woman who searches for the children she lost. To evaluate La Llorona’s cultural importance, it is necessary to consider her symbolic depth, generational longevity, and psychological impact. Through these criteria, it becomes clear that La Llorona remains a powerful and meaningful cultural figure.
________________


Body Paragraphs
Each body paragraph should be organized around one criterion and follow this general structure:
                                          * Topic sentence introducing the criterion

                                          * A claim about how the subject meets (or does not meet) the criterion

                                          * Evidence (examples, research, description, or quotations)

                                          * Analysis explaining how the evidence supports the claim

                                          * A reinforcing statement linking back to the overall judgment

Example Body Paragraph (Criterion: Symbolism)
 La Llorona is symbolically rich because she represents deep and persistent emotional and cultural anxieties. In many versions of the tale, her constant search for her lost children symbolizes grief, loss, and the consequences of moral transgression. These symbolic layers make her story emotionally resonant and ensure its continued relevance within communities. This symbolic depth is one reason La Llorona has endured as a meaningful cultural figure for generations.
________________


Conclusion
A strong conclusion should:
                                             * reaffirm your thesis and main evaluation,

                                             * emphasize the significance of your judgment, and

                                             * highlight what your evaluation reveals about the subject’s cultural, literary, or symbolic value.

Example Conclusion
 By examining La Llorona through the criteria of symbolic depth, cultural longevity, and psychological influence, it becomes evident that she is a culturally significant and enduring folkloric figure. Her story continues to shape identity and memory across generations, demonstrating the lasting power of monsters that embody community fears and unresolved historical trauma.
________________


A Visual Overview of the Evaluation Structure
Although presented here in text form, students may visualize the structure as:

Body Paragraph 3

Understanding What “Criteria” Means
Criteria are the standards, benchmarks, or measurable qualities you use to judge the effectiveness or value of a subject. In an evaluation essay, criteria must be:
                                                      * Relevant to the subject

                                                      * Measurable using textual or research-based evidence

                                                      * Clear and specific

                                                      * Debatable, not obvious or universally agreed upon

                                                      * Consistent throughout the essay

Criteria make your evaluation fair, logical, and academically grounded rather than personal or emotional.
Without criteria, an evaluation becomes a list of reactions. With criteria, it becomes an argument supported by analysis.
________________


Why Criteria Matter
Criteria are essential because they:
                                                         1. Establish the standards the subject is being measured against.

                                                         2. Shape the body paragraphs — one criterion becomes one paragraph.

                                                         3. Give your essay structure and clarity.

                                                         4. Show your reader that your evaluation is reasoned, not random.

                                                         5. Help you maintain focus, preventing summary or plot description.

Criteria are the foundation of the evaluation essay. You cannot make a judgment without them.
________________


Examples of Strong vs. Weak Criteria
Strong, Academic Criteria
These allow for rich analysis and evidence:
                                                            * Symbolic depth (Does the monster/text offer layered meaning?)

                                                            * Cultural relevance (Does it reflect meaningful cultural fears or values?)

                                                            * Psychological impact (Does it influence emotions or behavior?)

                                                            * Narrative function (Does it advance the story or theme effectively?)

                                                            * Originality (Is the portrayal fresh, innovative, or influential?)

                                                            * Social commentary (Does it critique, expose, or challenge something?)

                                                            * Representation (Does the text handle identity, trauma, or culture thoughtfully?)

These criteria work well for evaluating monsters, folklore, and literature.
________________


Weak or Vague Criteria
These lead to unsupported opinions or summary:
                                                               * “It was scary.”

                                                               * “It was boring.”

                                                               * “I liked it.”

                                                               * “It made sense.”

                                                               * “It was confusing.”

These are either personal preferences or overly general statements that cannot support strong analysis.
________________


Selecting Good Criteria
When choosing criteria, ask yourself:
                                                                  * What qualities matter most for evaluating this type of subject?
 Evaluating a monster requires different criteria than evaluating a poem or film scene.

                                                                  * What makes this subject meaningful or important?
 Is it culturally relevant? Symbolic? Historically significant?

                                                                  * What do scholars, folklorists, or critics say about similar subjects?
 Your research can guide your criteria.

                                                                  * What kind of evidence will you be able to use?
 Criteria should be chosen based on what you can support with text-based examples.

________________

A Three-Part Formula for Creating Good Criteria
To develop strong criteria, try the following approach:
                                                                                 1. Identify the subject’s purpose or role.
 What does it attempt to do or represent?

                                                                                 2. Determine what qualities are necessary for it to succeed.
 What makes a monster meaningful? What makes a story effective?

                                                                                 3. Turn those qualities into measurable standards.
 What can be analyzed, quoted, or supported with evidence?

Example:
If evaluating “The Yellow Wallpaper,” you might start with “It is a story about mental illness and confinement.”
From that, criteria could include:
                                                                                    * portrayal of psychological decline

                                                                                    * use of symbolism

                                                                                    * effectiveness of narrative structure

________________


Developing Your Own Criteria: A Step-by-Step Guide
                                                                                       1. Write down your chosen subject.

                                                                                       2. List all the qualities you think matter for evaluating it.

                                                                                       3. Cross out anything vague or based on personal preference.

                                                                                       4. Circle 3 qualities that:

                                                                                          * can be supported with evidence

                                                                                          * relate to the subject’s significance

                                                                                          * allow for strong analysis

                                                                                             5. Rewrite them as clear, academic criteria.

Different Types of Monsters & Cultural Artifacts to Evaluate
Understanding What Counts as a “Monster” or Cultural Artifact
In ENGL 1302, the term “monster” extends beyond literal creatures. A monster can be:
                                                                                                * a being (supernatural, folkloric, mythic),

                                                                                                * a symbolic figure (fear, grief, trauma),

                                                                                                * a social structure (a community that harms its own members), or

                                                                                                * a representation of cultural anxieties (e.g., technology, institutions, or traditions).

A cultural artifact may be any text, story, or object that carries meaning within a community. This can include:
                                                                                                   * folklore and oral stories

                                                                                                   * literary works

                                                                                                   * myths

                                                                                                   * rituals or practices

                                                                                                   * narrative tropes

                                                                                                   * symbolic locations

                                                                                                   * images or media (films, comics, etc.)

These subjects provide rich opportunities for evaluation because they allow you to analyze:
                                                                                                      * their symbolic function

                                                                                                      * cultural influence

                                                                                                      * narrative purpose

                                                                                                      * emotional or psychological impact

                                                                                                      * historical relevance

                                                                                                      * connections to identity, fear, or community storytelling

________________


Types of Monsters You May Evaluate
Below are categories of monsters that frequently appear in literature, culture, and your coursework.
________________


1. Folkloric Monsters
Folkloric monsters arise from oral traditions and reflect the values, fears, and histories of a community.
Examples:
                                                                                                         * La Llorona (grief, morality, loss)

                                                                                                         * El Cucuy / El Cuco (discipline, fear of disobedience)

                                                                                                         * The Donkey Lady (borderland trauma, injustice, haunting)

                                                                                                         * Chupacabra (fear of the unknown, modern mythmaking)

                                                                                                         * El Sombrerón (punishment for vanity or disobedience)

Topics for evaluation include:
                                                                                                            * Why the story has survived

                                                                                                            * How it influences behavior

                                                                                                            * Whether it adapts across generations

                                                                                                            * How it reflects cultural identity

                                                                                                            * How it symbolizes community fears

________________


2. Literary Monsters
These monsters are crafted through narrative and characterization in fiction.
Examples:
                                                                                                               * Frankenstein’s Creature (science, ethics, abandonment)

                                                                                                               * Dracula (desire, disease, foreignness, threat)

                                                                                                               * The Creature from “The Yellow Wallpaper” (mental illness, confinement)

                                                                                                               * The Lottery’s community (violence, conformity, tradition)

                                                                                                               * Grendel from Beowulf (outsider status, rage, identity)

Evaluation may involve:
                                                                                                                  * symbolic meaning

                                                                                                                  * narrative effectiveness

                                                                                                                  * commentary on human behavior

                                                                                                                  * representation of fear or desire

                                                                                                                  * originality and influence

________________


3. Pop Culture Monsters
Modern films, streaming series, video games, and graphic novels introduce monsters that comment on contemporary issues.
Examples:
                                                                                                                     * Pennywise (childhood trauma, predatory evil)

                                                                                                                     * Xenomorph from Alien (body horror, violation, survival)

                                                                                                                     * Vecna from Stranger Things (trauma, memory, repression)

                                                                                                                     * The Babadook (grief, mental illness)

                                                                                                                     * Slenderman (internet-age fear, anonymity)

These monsters allow for evaluation of:
                                                                                                                        * visual symbolism

                                                                                                                        * social commentary

                                                                                                                        * impact on modern audiences

                                                                                                                        * genre innovation

                                                                                                                        * psychological depth

________________


4. Symbolic or Abstract Monsters
Not all monsters have a body. Some are metaphors for harmful forces or structures.
Examples:
                                                                                                                           * Government secrecy (as in MK-Ultra or Hawkins Lab)

                                                                                                                           * Community hysteria (Satanic Panic, rumor-based fear)

                                                                                                                           * Silence or repression (trauma that is not acknowledged)

                                                                                                                           * Addiction, grief, or guilt (personified or thematic)

                                                                                                                           * Oppression or injustice (manifested through actions)

Evaluation might focus on:
                                                                                                                              * effectiveness of symbolism

                                                                                                                              * the subject’s relevance today

                                                                                                                              * its emotional or psychological impact

                                                                                                                              * how the text uses metaphor to represent social issues

Types of Cultural Artifacts Suitable for Evaluation
1. Folklore and Oral Narratives
Stories passed from generation to generation, often evolving with time.
Examples:
                                                                                                                                 * borderland legends

                                                                                                                                 * creation myths

                                                                                                                                 * ghost stories

Evaluation considerations:

How to Decide Whether a Monster or Artifact is Good for Evaluation
A subject is strong for evaluation if:
                                                                                                                                                         * it has depth, not just surface-level meaning

                                                                                                                                                         * it carries cultural, symbolic, or emotional significance

                                                                                                                                                         * people interpret it in different ways

                                                                                                                                                         * it has a clear purpose or function you can analyze

                                                                                                                                                         * there is enough evidence to support your criteria

                                                                                                                                                         * it connects to broader themes like fear, culture, or identity

If the subject is too simple, too new, or has little cultural meaning, evaluation will be limited.

Reading Strategies for Evaluation Texts
Why Reading for Evaluation Is Different
Reading for evaluation requires a different mindset than reading for plot or enjoyment. Instead of asking, “What happens?”, you must learn to ask questions like:
                                                                                                                                                               * What is the text trying to accomplish?

                                                                                                                                                               * How effectively does it achieve this purpose?

                                                                                                                                                               * What choices did the creator make, and why?

                                                                                                                                                               * What cultural or symbolic work does the monster/text perform?

                                                                                                                                                               * What patterns or contradictions shape the story’s impact?

Evaluation begins with attentive, analytical reading, not summary.
________________


Key Reading Behaviors for Evaluation
1. Read with a Focus on Purpose
Ask yourself what the text, story, or monster is meant to do.
                                                                                                                                                                  * Is it meant to frighten?

                                                                                                                                                                  * To warn?

                                                                                                                                                                  * To reflect trauma or cultural fear?

                                                                                                                                                                  * To entertain?

                                                                                                                                                                  * To teach a moral lesson?

Purpose becomes the foundation for your criteria.
Example:
 In “The Lottery,” the ritual is not meant to scare the reader like a horror monster, but to critique conformity and blind tradition.
________________


2. Annotate for Meaning, Not Plot
Highlight or note:
                                                                                                                                                                     * symbolism

                                                                                                                                                                     * themes

                                                                                                                                                                     * repeated images or phrases

                                                                                                                                                                     * contradictions

                                                                                                                                                                     * narrative structure

                                                                                                                                                                     * tone or mood

                                                                                                                                                                     * emotional responses

                                                                                                                                                                     * cultural references

These are the materials you will use to build your evaluation.
________________


3. Identify Patterns
Patterns show what the text emphasizes.
Look for:
                                                                                                                                                                        * repeated behaviors

                                                                                                                                                                        * visual motifs

                                                                                                                                                                        * descriptive choices

                                                                                                                                                                        * consistent emotional tones

                                                                                                                                                                        * narrative cycles

Example:
 La Llorona always cries along the river, linking water to memory and grief.
Patterns often become criteria or evidence.
________________


4. Notice Gaps, Silences, or Ambiguities
What the text doesn’t say is often as important as what it does.
Ask:
                                                                                                                                                                           * What remains unspoken?

                                                                                                                                                                           * What does the community ignore?

                                                                                                                                                                           * What is hinted at but not explained?

                                                                                                                                                                           * Where does the story choose silence?

Ambiguities can reveal deeper symbolic meaning.
________________


5. Consider the Cultural or Historical Context
A monster or story often reflects:
                                                                                                                                                                              * historical fears

                                                                                                                                                                              * generational trauma

                                                                                                                                                                              * political anxieties

                                                                                                                                                                              * cultural identity

                                                                                                                                                                              * regional differences

Context helps you determine whether a monster or text is culturally relevant or symbolically important.
Example:
 The Chupacabra emerges in periods of economic uncertainty, reflecting modern anxieties about survival and the unknown.
________________


6. Ask Evaluative Questions as You Read
Instead of “What happened next?”, try:
                                                                                                                                                                                 * How effectively is fear created?

                                                                                                                                                                                 * What makes this story memorable or culturally important?

                                                                                                                                                                                 * Does the text rely on stereotypes or challenge them?

                                                                                                                                                                                 * Does this monster represent deeper anxieties?

                                                                                                                                                                                 * What strengths or weaknesses appear in the narrative?

                                                                                                                                                                                 * Is the symbolism clear, subtle, or inconsistent?

These questions lead directly to criteria and judgments.
________________


7. Pay Attention to Emotional and Psychological Impact
Your emotional response matters—but not as a reaction.
Analyze:
                                                                                                                                                                                    * What triggered the response?

                                                                                                                                                                                    * How did the creator use imagery, pacing, or symbolism to create it?

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Is the emotional effect intentional or accidental?

In evaluation, emotional response becomes evidence, not the argument itself.
________________


Reading Strategies by Type of Text
If reading folklore:
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Note variations or retellings

                                                                                                                                                                                       * Examine the structure of oral storytelling

                                                                                                                                                                                       * Consider the role of fear, morality, or community memory

                                                                                                                                                                                       * Highlight cultural elements tied to geography (e.g., South Texas borderlands)

If reading literary fiction:
                                                                                                                                                                                          * Analyze the narrator

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Identify themes

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Look closely at figurative language

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Evaluate how characters represent ideas or conflicts

If reading pop culture media:
                                                                                                                                                                                             * Observe visual choices

                                                                                                                                                                                             * Consider allegory or commentary

                                                                                                                                                                                             * Pay attention to atmosphere, pacing, and tone

                                                                                                                                                                                             * Evaluate representation of marginalized characters or communities

If reading symbolic or abstract monsters:
                                                                                                                                                                                                * Identify what the monster stands in for

                                                                                                                                                                                                * Trace cause-and-effect reasoning

                                                                                                                                                                                                * Look for thematic patterns, not physical ones

________________


A Simple Annotation System for Evaluation
Encourage students to mark texts in a structured way:
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * C = Criterion-related (ideas that could become standards)

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * E = Evidence (quotes, images, descriptions)

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * S = Symbol (objects, animals, recurring images)

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * M = Meaning (possible interpretations)

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Q = Question (points of confusion or inquiry)

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * P = Pattern (repetition, cycles)

This prevents students from slipping into plot summary and keeps their reading focused on evaluation.
________________

This activity prepares students for generating criteria in their essay.

The following reading questions help you analyze a monster, folklore text, literary work, or cultural artifact for your Evaluation Essay. Each section includes:

I. Purpose and Function Questions
Definition
These questions help you identify why the text or monster exists and what it is trying to do. Purpose reveals the creator’s intention, the cultural meaning, or the narrative function.
Why This Matters
Evaluating a subject requires knowing what it attempts to achieve. A monster meant to warn will be judged using different criteria than a monster meant to scare or symbolize trauma.
Example Applications
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * La Llorona: Purpose may be to warn children, represent grief, or preserve memory.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Frankenstein’s Creature: Purpose may be to critique scientific ambition.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * “The Lottery”: Purpose may be to reveal the dangers of blind tradition.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Vecna: Purpose may be to symbolize trauma and emotional repression.

Guiding Questions
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    1. What is the text or monster trying to accomplish?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    2. What cultural or psychological work does it perform?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    3. Who is the intended audience or community?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    4. What message, warning, or fear does it convey?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    5. Does it challenge or reinforce cultural expectations?

________________


II. Structure, Techniques, and Representation Questions
Definition
These questions analyze how the text or monster is presented through narrative structure, literary devices, visual strategies, symbolism, and characterization.
Why This Matters
The way a story is constructed—its pacing, imagery, perspective—directly impacts how effectively it communicates meaning.
Example Applications
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Symbolism:
 The river in La Llorona represents memory and grief.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Structure:
 “The Lottery” delays the revelation of violence to critique normalized brutality.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Technique:
 The Yellow Wallpaper uses first-person narration to show mental decline.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Representation:
 Pennywise shapeshifts into individualized fears, reinforcing his symbolic role.

Guiding Questions
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          6. What narrative strategies stand out?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          7. What symbols appear, and what might they represent?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          8. How does the setting contribute to mood or meaning?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          9. How does visual design or descriptive imagery affect interpretation?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          10. Are there ambiguities or gaps that add meaning?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          11. How do other characters react to the monster or symbolic force?

III. Effectiveness, Impact, and Value Questions
Definition
These questions help you judge how well the text or monster fulfills its purpose. This is the heart of evaluation.
Why This Matters
Your essay must answer whether the subject is effective—and these questions help you measure success using evidence.
Example Applications
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Effectiveness:
 Vecna is effective as a monster because he uses victims’ memories against them.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Impact:
 “The Lottery” is impactful because its calm tone contrasts with its violent ending.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Value:
 La Llorona holds cultural value due to her generational presence in storytelling.

Sample Evaluation of a Literary Text
The sample below demonstrates how to use criteria, evidence, and reasoning to evaluate a literary work. Read it carefully to understand how an evaluation paragraph moves beyond summary to present a judgment supported by analysis.
This example evaluates Shirley Jackson’s short story “The Lottery.”
________________


Sample Evaluation Paragraph
Shirley Jackson’s “The Lottery” is an effective critique of blind conformity because it uses symbolic objects, understated narrative tone, and a shocking final ritual to expose the brutality hidden within ordinary communities. One of the story’s strongest symbolic elements is the black box, which represents tradition maintained without question. Jackson describes the box as “splintered” and “fading” (Jackson), yet the townspeople refuse to replace it because they fear altering the ritual’s meaning. This detail supports the criterion of symbolic depth by illustrating how the community protects tradition even when the object itself is deteriorating. Additionally, the story’s calm and detached tone intensifies the critique of conformity. Jackson writes about the preparation for the lottery with the same neutrality as one might describe a summer picnic. This controlled tone reinforces the idea that violence becomes normalized when it is woven into everyday life. The story’s conclusion—Tessie Hutchinson’s stoning—provides the final evidence that the community’s commitment to tradition outweighs their commitment to morality. Because Jackson uses symbolism, tone, and an escalating narrative structure to reveal the dangers of unexamined practices, “The Lottery” succeeds as a culturally resonant and deeply unsettling critique of conformity.
________________


Commentary on the Sample Paragraph
To help you understand how an evaluation paragraph works, the following breakdown identifies where each component appears.
1. Judgment-Based Topic Sentence
 “Shirley Jackson’s ‘The Lottery’ is an effective critique of blind conformity…”
This makes a clear judgment and names the criteria (symbolic objects, tone, final ritual).
2. Criterion #1: Symbolic Depth
 “The black box… represents tradition maintained without question.”
Here, the paragraph identifies a criterion and provides a symbolic interpretation.
3. Evidence for Symbolism
 “‘splintered’ and ‘fading’ (Jackson)”
Direct textual evidence supports the evaluation.
4. Reasoning
 “This detail supports the criterion… because the community protects tradition even when the object itself is deteriorating.”
The paragraph explains how the evidence proves the point.
5. Criterion #2: Narrative Tone
 “Calm and detached tone intensifies the critique of conformity.”
Another criterion anchors the next section of analysis.
6. Evidence for Tone
 Neutral descriptions of the lottery preparation show how ordinary the ritual seems.
7. Criterion #3: Narrative Structure / Climactic Ritual
 “The story’s conclusion… provides the final evidence…”
This shows how the ending reinforces the judgment.
8. Final Reinforcing Judgment
 “…succeeds as a culturally resonant and deeply unsettling critique…”
The paragraph restates the overarching judgment.

Sample Evaluation of a Cultural Monster
The following sample demonstrates how to evaluate a folkloric or cultural monster using clear criteria, specific evidence, and logical reasoning. Cultural monsters often function as mirrors of collective fears, moral lessons, or regional identities. This example evaluates La Llorona, one of the most enduring figures in Mexican and Tejano folklore.
________________


Sample Evaluation Paragraph
La Llorona is a culturally significant and emotionally powerful monster because her story symbolizes generational grief, reinforces community warnings about moral transgression, and adapts across regions to reflect local fears. One of the clearest indicators of her cultural importance is her symbolic connection to grief and loss. In many versions of the tale, La Llorona wanders along rivers searching for the children she drowned, her cries echoing unresolved sorrow. This recurring image supports the criterion of symbolic depth because it connects her haunting to universal experiences of mourning and regret. Additionally, La Llorona’s story functions as a cautionary tale used by families to reinforce moral boundaries and protect children from danger. Parents often retell the legend as a warning against wandering out at night or disobeying elders, demonstrating her role in shaping behavior through fear. Finally, the numerous variations of La Llorona found throughout Latin America show her adaptability, which strengthens her cultural longevity. Each region emphasizes different themes—betrayal, jealousy, tragedy, colonial trauma—revealing how the legend evolves while maintaining its emotional core. Through symbolic complexity, generational function, and regional adaptability, La Llorona remains an effective and enduring cultural monster whose story continues to shape identity and memory in many communities.
________________

2. Criterion #1 — Symbolic Depth

3. Evidence for Symbolism
 “In many versions of the tale, La Llorona wanders along rivers searching for her children…”
This detail appears in multiple retellings and supports a universal emotional theme.
4. Reasoning
 “This recurring image… connects her haunting to universal experiences of mourning and regret.”
The writer explains why the evidence matters.
5. Criterion #2 — Community Function / Moral Warning
 Used to examine how the legend shapes behavior and cultural boundaries.
6. Evidence for Community Function
 Parents retelling the story to prevent dangerous behavior.
7. Criterion #3 — Adaptability and Regional Variations
 The story changes across regions yet retains its core meaning, showing cultural longevity.
8. Final Reinforcing Judgment
 La Llorona “remains an effective and enduring cultural monster…”
The paragraph ends by reaffirming the evaluation.

Evaluation Essay Writing Guide
The Evaluation Essay requires you to make an informed, defensible judgment about a text, monster, folklore figure, or cultural artifact. This guide walks you through each step of the writing process—from crafting a thesis to organizing body paragraphs—so you can develop a clear, focused, analytical essay.
________________


Understanding the Goal of the Evaluation Essay
Before you begin writing, you should understand what the evaluation essay is not. It is not:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * a summary of the story,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * a list of likes and dislikes,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * a retelling of folklore, nor

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * a personal reaction.

Instead, it is an essay that:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       1. Presents a judgment,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       2. Supports that judgment using criteria, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       3. Proves that judgment with evidence and reasoning.

Your goal is to help your reader understand the significance, effectiveness, or cultural value of the subject you are evaluating.
________________


Step 1: Choosing a Strong Subject
Choose a subject that:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * has enough complexity for analysis,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * has symbolic or cultural meaning,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * offers multiple examples or passages you can use as evidence, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * relates to themes such as fear, culture, trauma, memory, identity, or community.

Examples of strong subjects include:
La Llorona, Frankenstein’s Creature, Pennywise, “The Lottery,” “The Yellow Wallpaper,” the Donkey Lady, the Chupacabra, Vecna, and other symbolic or folkloric figures.
Weak subjects include:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * monsters with no cultural background,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * texts without symbolic layers, or

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * subjects that rely only on visual shock with no deeper analysis.

________________


Step 2: Selecting Three Criteria
Your entire essay depends on the quality of your criteria.
Criteria should be:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * clear,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * debatable,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * specific,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * academically appropriate, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * supported by evidence.

Examples of strong criteria:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * symbolic depth

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * cultural relevance

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * narrative function

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * psychological impact

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * moral or ethical commentary

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * originality

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * longevity across generations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * representation of trauma or memory

Remember: each criterion becomes one body paragraph.
________________


Step 3: Writing a Judgment-Based Thesis
Your thesis should do three things:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      1. Identify the subject.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      2. Make a judgment (effective, ineffective, meaningful, limited, etc.).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      3. Preview the criteria you will use to support your judgment.

Thesis Formula
Subject + Judgment + Criteria 1, 2, and 3
Example Theses
Example 1 (Folklore):
 La Llorona is an effective cultural monster because she symbolizes generational grief, reinforces moral lessons, and adapts across different regions to reflect local fears.
Example 2 (Literary):
 “The Lottery” succeeds as a critique of conformity due to its symbolic objects, understated tone, and shocking final ritual.
Example 3 (Pop Culture Monster):
 Pennywise remains one of modern horror’s most unsettling figures because of his psychological manipulation, symbolic representation of childhood fear, and narrative pattern of preying on the vulnerable.
Weak Thesis Example:
“La Llorona is interesting because she is scary.”
(This lacks criteria, judgment, and academic clarity.)
________________


Step 4: Structuring Your Body Paragraphs
Each body paragraph must focus on one criterion and follow a clear structure.
Body Paragraph Structure
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         1. Topic Sentence

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Introduces the criterion

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Makes a mini-judgment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               2. Explanation of the Criterion

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Clarifies what the criterion means in the context of this subject

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     3. Evidence

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Quote, scene, description, retelling, or scholarly reference

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           4. Reasoning/Analysis

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Explains how the evidence supports your criterion

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Shows why the criterion proves your overall judgment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 5. Link Back

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Reinforces the subject’s effectiveness or importance

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Transitions to the next paragraph

Example Topic Sentences
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * “La Llorona exhibits deep symbolic meaning through her association with water and grief.”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * “Frankenstein’s Creature contributes to the novel’s ethical complexity by revealing the consequences of irresponsible creation.”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * “Pennywise’s psychological manipulation is central to his effectiveness as a modern horror monster.”

Evidence Tips
Choose evidence that:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * is specific and meaningful,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * supports your criterion directly,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * allows for interpretation or explanation, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * avoids long block quotes unless necessary.

________________


Step 5: Writing the Introduction
A strong introduction:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * provides context about the subject,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * briefly describes the cultural, historical, or literary background,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * previews the criteria, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * ends with a thesis.

Introduction Template
Introduce subject → Provide brief context → State purpose → Present criteria → Thesis
Example Introduction Opening
Stories of monsters often reveal the cultural fears and values of the communities that tell them. One of the most enduring figures in Mexican and Tejano folklore is La Llorona…
________________


Step 6: Writing the Conclusion
A strong conclusion:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * revisits your thesis and judgment,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * reflects on the broader cultural or symbolic significance,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * shows what your evaluation reveals about the subject’s importance.

Avoid:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * repeating the thesis word-for-word,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * introducing new evidence,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * ending abruptly.

Conclusion Prompts
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Why does your evaluation matter?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * What continues to make this monster or text relevant today?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * What cultural or symbolic role does it play beyond the story itself?

________________


Step 7: Integrating MLA Format
Your essay must:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * follow MLA 9 format,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * include in-text citations for quotes or paraphrases,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * include a Works Cited page.

This will be covered in detail later in Section I’s MLA appendix.

Checklist for a Strong Evaluation Essay

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Your thesis makes a clear, defensible judgment.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * You use three clear academic criteria.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Your paragraphs follow a structured format.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Your evidence is specific and meaningful.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Your analysis explains how and why the evidence supports the criterion.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * You avoid summary and personal reaction.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * You maintain academic tone and clarity.

How to Choose Your Topic
Choose a subject that:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * provides enough symbolic or cultural complexity to support three criteria,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * allows for at least two credible sources,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * connects with your interests or background,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * offers clear evidence for analysis, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * relates to themes of fear, identity, cultural memory, or narrative meaning.

If you are unsure whether your subject works for evaluation, bring it to class or office hours for approval before drafting your thesis.


Research is a central component of the Evaluation Essay. Strong evaluation depends on credible evidence, not assumptions or unsupported claims. This section provides guidance on finding reliable sources, identifying relevant material, and incorporating research into your essay effectively.
Your research must help you:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * understand the cultural or historical background of your subject,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * support your criteria with credible information,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * strengthen your analysis and reasoning, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * demonstrate academic responsibility through MLA citation.

________________


Why Research Matters in an Evaluation Essay
Evaluation is not based on personal preference. Research allows you to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 1. Support your criteria with cultural, historical, psychological, or literary analysis.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 2. Clarify the origins or variations of a folklore story.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 3. Investigate symbolism used by the author or the community.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 4. Understand audience reception, adaptations, and interpretations.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 5. Ground your judgment in verifiable information.

Your goal is to blend your analytical voice with credible sources to produce a balanced academic evaluation.

I. Required Research for Essay 1
Your Evaluation Essay must include:
1. A primary source
This is the text or monster you are evaluating, such as:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * the folklore versions of La Llorona or El Cucuy,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * the literary text (“The Lottery,” “The Yellow Wallpaper,” etc.),

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * the monster itself (Pennywise, Vecna, Donkey Lady),

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * the cultural artifact (ritual, tradition, symbol).

2. At least one secondary source
This is research that helps explain or contextualize your subject:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * scholarly articles

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * cultural analyses

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * psychology or folklore studies

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * news features on folklore or monster culture

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * background sources (Smithsonian, NPR, Texas Observer, BBC)

You may use more than one secondary source.

Step 3: Evaluate Source Credibility
Ask:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Is the author reputable?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Is the publication reliable (academic, journalistic, or institutional)?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Does it use evidence?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Does it offer analysis, not just summary?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Is the information current (if needed)?

If a source answers yes to most of these questions, it is likely credible.

IV. Taking Notes and Organizing Research
When you find useful material, record:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * the author

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * the title

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * key quotes

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * paraphrased ideas

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * examples you can use as evidence

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * how the information connects to your criteria

Sample Note-Taking Format
Source: Smithsonian Folklife – “La Llorona: The Weeping Woman”
Key Quote: “La Llorona’s cries echo unresolved grief that spans generations.”
Connection: Supports criterion of symbolic depth.
Possible Use: Body Paragraph 1 evidence.

V. Using Research in Your Essay
Use your secondary sources to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * provide cultural background,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * explain symbolic meaning,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * support your criteria,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * reinforce your judgment.

Do not let sources take over your essay.
Your analysis should remain the central voice.
Example of Strong Source Integration
According to the Smithsonian Folklife Center, La Llorona’s cries “echo unresolved grief passed down through generations.” This supports the criterion of symbolic depth because her haunting becomes a representation of inherited sorrow rather than a simple ghost story.
Weak Example
La Llorona is sad in the story. The Smithsonian says she is grieving.
(This does not explain why the evidence matters.)

VI. Building Your Works Cited Page
Every source you use—primary or secondary—must appear on a Works Cited page.
Follow MLA 9 guidelines:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * alphabetize by author’s last name

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * use hanging indent

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * include URLs for online sources

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * double-space throughout

We will cover MLA formatting in detail later in the MLA Appendix of this textbook.

VII. Common Research Mistakes to Avoid
Avoid:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * choosing sources with no author,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * relying on plot summaries instead of analysis,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * using too many direct quotes without explanation,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * citing Wikipedia as a source,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * failing to incorporate research into analysis,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * selecting criteria before doing any reading.

Your criteria should be shaped by your reading and your research—not by assumptions.


Keyword Search Strategies
Effective research begins with the ability to generate strong, flexible, and targeted keywords. Academic databases, scholarly websites, and cultural archives rely on keywords to help you locate relevant sources.
Knowing how to select and combine keywords allows you to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * find higher-quality evidence,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * avoid irrelevant search results,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * locate deeper symbolic or cultural interpretations,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * refine your criteria,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * expand your understanding of your subject.

This section teaches you how to build, combine, and refine keywords based on your chosen monster, literary work, or cultural artifact.
________________


I. Why Keywords Matter
Good keywords lead you to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * scholarly articles,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * credible folklore sources,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * cultural commentary,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * psychological or symbolic interpretation,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * historical context,

IV. Using Boolean Operators to Improve Searches
Boolean operators help you narrow or expand search results.
1. AND
Narrows search by requiring both terms.
“La Llorona AND symbolism”
2. OR
Expands search by including either term.
“La Llorona OR Weeping Woman”
3. NOT
Excludes unwanted results.
“Slenderman NOT Creepypasta”
4. Quotation Marks
Finds exact phrases.
“female monster symbolism”
5. Asterisk (*)
Searches word variations.
monst* → monster, monstrous, monstrosity

Utilizing Multiple Data Collection Methods
Strong evaluation writing depends on gathering information from multiple angles. Instead of relying on a single text or summary, you should explore your subject through several data collection methods. These methods will help you:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * deepen your understanding of cultural or historical context,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * identify symbolic patterns and variations,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * support your criteria with richer evidence, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * strengthen your academic credibility.

This section explains the key data collection methods you may use and provides activities to help you apply them to your chosen monster or text.
________________


I. Method 1: Primary Text Analysis
Definition
Examining the original story, folklore version, literary text, or visual source closely to identify symbolic, cultural, or narrative patterns.
What to Look For
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * key descriptive details

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * patterns or repetitions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * symbolic objects or locations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * character reactions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * narrative structure

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * tone or mood

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * emotional impact

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * missing or ambiguous details

Examples
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * La Llorona: repeated weeping near water → symbol of generational grief

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * “The Lottery”: black box → symbol of tradition and unquestioned violence

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Vecna: connection to memory → embodiment of unresolved trauma

________________


II. Method 2: Secondary Texts and Critical Commentary
Definition
Using scholarly, journalistic, folkloric, or psychological sources that interpret or contextualize the subject.
What to Look For
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * explanations of folklore origins

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * literary analysis

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * cultural history

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * interpretations of symbols

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * psychological theories tied to fear or trauma

Examples
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Smithsonian or Texas Observer articles discussing folklore

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Scholarly essays analyzing horror or trauma

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Articles on conformity and social psychology related to “The Lottery”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Commentary on grief and mental health in The Babadook

III. Method 3: Comparative Data (Variants and Versions)
Definition
Examining multiple versions or adaptations of a folklore story, monster narrative, or literary motif to identify patterns or cultural shifts.
What to Look For
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * differences in characterization

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * regional adaptations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * changes in symbolism over time

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * themes that remain consistent across versions

Examples
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * La Llorona versions vary across Mexico, New Mexico, and Texas

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Donkey Lady legends differ between San Antonio and surrounding counties

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Slenderman narratives shift between platforms and generations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Frankenstein adaptations shift from sympathy to horror

Comparative analysis strengthens your evaluation by showing how the subject evolves and why.

IV. Method 4: Contextual and Cultural Research
Definition
Gathering information about the historical, social, or cultural context in which the monster or text was created or continues to circulate.
What to Look For
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * historical events

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * community fears

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * generational trauma

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * cultural or moral expectations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * regional identity and storytelling traditions

Examples
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * “The Lottery” written during post-war American anxiety

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * La Llorona connected to colonial history and maternal grief

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Slenderman emerging from internet-age fears

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Vecna tied to psychological trauma and memory repression

Context helps explain why your subject matters and strengthens cultural relevance as a criterion.

V. Method 5: Audience Reception and Community Perspectives
Definition
Understanding how a monster or text is received, used, or interpreted by the community or audience.
Where to Look
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * folklore retellings

Analyzing Data
Once you have collected information from primary texts, secondary sources, cultural context, audience reception, and comparative versions, the next step is data analysis. Analysis is the process of interpreting the information you gathered and explaining why it matters for your evaluation.
Data analysis transforms:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * observations into interpretations,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * examples into evidence, and

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * details into analytical claims that support your criteria.

This page guides you through the process of analyzing your data so that your Evaluation Essay shows depth, clarity, and academic reasoning.
________________


I. What Does It Mean to Analyze Data?
Definition
Analysis is the process of breaking down information and explaining how it supports your evaluation.
What Analysis Is Not
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * repeating the quote

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * summarizing the folklore or plot

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * describing what happens

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * listing facts without interpretation

What Analysis Is
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * interpreting meaning

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * connecting evidence to your criterion

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * explaining how the evidence proves your judgment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * identifying cultural, symbolic, or emotional significance

Data becomes meaningful only when you analyze it.
________________


II. The Analysis Formula
Use this simple formula to guide your analysis:
1. Present the Evidence
A quote, detail, description, or observation.
2. Interpret the Evidence
Explain what it means or represents.
3. Connect to the Criterion
Show how the evidence supports the standard you’re evaluating.
4. Reinforce Your Judgment
Explain how this strengthens (or weakens) your final evaluation.
________________


III. Examples of Strong Data Analysis
Example 1 — Folklore (La Llorona)
Evidence:
 Many versions describe La Llorona wandering riversides, crying for her lost children.
Analysis:
 This recurring image symbolizes generational grief and unresolved trauma. The repetition of water locations reinforces her connection to memory and sorrow.
Connection to Criterion:
 This supports the criterion of symbolic depth.
Reinforcement of Judgment:
 La Llorona is effective as a cultural monster because her symbolism expresses emotional and historical wounds shared across communities.
________________


Example 2 — Literary Text (“The Lottery”)
Evidence:
 The black box is described as “faded” and “splintered.”
Analysis:
 The damaged condition of the box suggests the erosion of tradition, yet the community refuses to replace it. This contrast highlights their irrational commitment to ritual.
Connection to Criterion:
 This supports the criterion of social commentary.
Reinforcement of Judgment:
 The symbol strengthens the story’s critique of conformity.
________________


Example 3 — Pop Culture Monster (Vecna)
Evidence:
 Vecna preys on characters’ buried memories.
Analysis:
 His attacks embody the psychological reality that unprocessed trauma resurfaces with destructive force.
Criterion:
 Representation of trauma.
Judgment:
 Vecna is an effective modern horror symbol because he reflects real emotional struggles.

Connecting Theory to Practice
Theories help us interpret texts, monsters, and cultural artifacts with greater depth. In academic writing, theory provides a lens—a structured way of seeing meaning that might not be immediately obvious.
In your Evaluation Essay, theoretical frameworks support your analysis by helping you:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * interpret symbolism,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * understand cultural context,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * explore psychological meaning,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * identify patterns in storytelling,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * support your criteria with credible reasoning.

This section explains several accessible theoretical approaches and shows you how to apply them effectively.
________________


I. What Does “Theory” Mean in ENGL 1302?
Definition
A theory is a structured idea or framework scholars use to interpret texts and cultural phenomena.
Purpose in Evaluation
Theory helps you:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 1. explain why your criteria matter,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 2. interpret symbolism and cultural meaning,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 3. connect your ideas to academic conversations,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 4. strengthen the sophistication of your essay,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 5. support a deeper judgment about your subject.

Using theory does not mean writing like a scholar. It means applying a lens to help clarify meaning.
________________


II. Monster Theory (Jeffrey Jerome Cohen)
Cohen’s “Monster Culture (Seven Theses)” is one of the most useful theoretical frameworks for evaluating monsters.
Key Concepts You Can Use
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Thesis I: The Monster’s Body Is a Cultural Body
 Monsters reflect cultural fears, desires, and anxieties.
Example: La Llorona reflects generational grief and cultural memory.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Thesis II: The Monster Always Escapes
 Monsters return in new forms across time and communities.
Example: Variations of Chupacabra adapt to new fears.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Thesis VI: Fear of the Monster Is Really a Kind of Desire
 Monsters reveal what fascinates or attracts us even as they frighten us.
Example: Slenderman embodies both fear of the unknown and the thrill of mystery.

How to Apply Monster Theory
Use it to explain:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * why your monster is culturally relevant,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * how it reflects community fear,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * how it adapts across time,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * why it continues to appear in new versions.

Example:
 Cohen’s Thesis I supports the criterion of cultural relevance because La Llorona’s body embodies the emotional wounds of her community.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Collective identity (shared beliefs or values)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Regional storytelling (how place shapes narrative)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Folklore as moral instruction

Application Example
The Donkey Lady functions as a reflection of South Texas identity and local memory, aligning with cultural theory about regional storytelling.
Use cultural theory to strengthen criteria such as:

Writing the Evaluation Introduction
The introduction to your Evaluation Essay establishes the foundation of your argument. It prepares your reader to understand what you are evaluating, why this evaluation matters, and how you will judge the subject. A strong introduction demonstrates academic tone, clarity, and purpose.
This section guides you through writing an effective introduction using specific strategies, examples, and templates.
________________


I. Purpose of the Evaluation Introduction
The introduction should:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           1. Introduce the subject (a monster, cultural artifact, or literary text).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           2. Provide brief context (folkloric background, publication details, cultural significance, or narrative setting).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           3. State the purpose of the evaluation (what you are examining and why).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           4. Preview the criteria that will guide your judgment.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           5. End with a clear, judgment-based thesis.

Avoid summarizing the entire story or describing every version of the folklore. The introduction should be brief, focused, and analytical.
________________


II. Components of a Strong Evaluation Introduction
1. Opening Context or Hook
Provide a general statement that introduces the cultural or thematic importance of monsters, folklore, literature, or symbolic narratives.
Examples:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Monsters often reflect the fears and desires of the communities that create them.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Folklore carries emotional and historical meaning through generations.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Literary monsters often symbolize psychological, cultural, or social conflicts.

The goal is to ease your reader into the subject without overloading them with summary.
________________


2. Background on the Subject
Introduce the specific monster or text you will evaluate.
Keep this background brief—one or two sentences.
Examples:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * One of the most enduring figures in Mexican and Tejano folklore is La Llorona, a weeping woman who roams riversides in search of her lost children.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Shirley Jackson’s “The Lottery,” first published in 1948, portrays an annual ritual performed with unsettling normalcy.

________________


3. Purpose of the Evaluation
Explain why evaluating this subject is meaningful.
Examples:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Understanding how La Llorona functions in folklore reveals how communities confront grief and generational trauma.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Evaluating “The Lottery” allows readers to examine how tradition influences moral choices.

________________


4. Preview of Criteria
Briefly identify the standards you will use to judge the subject.
These should be broad enough to introduce but specific enough to frame your argument.
Examples:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * symbolic depth, cultural relevance, emotional impact

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * narrative structure, tone, and social commentary

________________


5. Thesis Statement
Your thesis must include:

II. MLA In-Text Citations in Evaluation Writing
1. Quoting a Literary Text
Format: (Author Last Name Page Number)
Example:
The narrator describes the box as “splintered” and “faded” (Jackson 1).
2. Paraphrasing or summarizing a literary moment
Format is the same: (Author Page Number)
Example:
The community protects the ritual without question (Jackson 2).
________________


3. Citing Folklore with No Page Numbers or No Single Author
Most folklore is not “authored” in a traditional sense. Instead, you cite the source providing the version.
Format: (Source Title)
Example:
In many accounts, La Llorona wanders riverbanks crying for her children (“La Llorona: The Weeping Woman”).
If the source is an article with an author, cite the author:
Example:
Her haunting reflects generational sorrow (Torres).
________________


4. Citing Research Articles
Format: (Author Last Name Page Number) — if page numbers exist.
If no page numbers: (Author Last Name)
Example:
The legend adapts to reflect community anxieties (Martinez 4).
or
The legend adapts to reflect community anxieties (Martinez).

5. Citing Scenes from Film or Television
MLA recommends citing time stamps.
Format: (“Episode Title” 00:32:10–00:32:42)

Example 1: Folklore Evaluation Paragraph
Paragraph:
 La Llorona demonstrates strong symbolic depth through her connection to water and grief. Symbolic depth refers to the multiple layers of meaning a figure or object carries within a cultural narrative. In many versions of the tale, La Llorona is described wandering riverbanks, crying out for the children she drowned (“La Llorona: The Weeping Woman”). This recurring image symbolizes generational sorrow and unresolved trauma, suggesting that grief flows continuously within the communities that share her story. The river becomes a reminder of loss that cannot be contained or forgotten, reinforcing the emotional weight of her narrative. Because this symbolism reveals deeper cultural meaning beyond the surface-level haunting, La Llorona meets the criterion of symbolic depth. This symbolic resonance strengthens her role as an effective cultural monster.

Example 2: Literary Text Evaluation Paragraph
Paragraph:
 “The Lottery” succeeds as a critique of conformity because Jackson’s use of tone makes the violence appear disturbingly ordinary. Tone refers to the author’s attitude toward the subject, shown through word choice and narrative style. Jackson describes the black box as “splintered” and “faded,” yet the villagers refuse to replace it (Jackson 1). This contrast highlights their unquestioned commitment to tradition, even when the object representing that tradition is deteriorating. The neutral tone used to describe the townspeople’s preparations makes the eventual violence feel normalized, reinforcing how easily communities can accept harmful practices. This supports the criterion of social commentary by revealing the dangers of blind obedience. Therefore, Jackson’s tone strengthens the story’s effectiveness as a cultural critique.

Writing the Evaluation Conclusion
The conclusion of your Evaluation Essay should reinforce your overall judgment, deepen your reader’s understanding of the subject’s significance, and offer a meaningful final insight. Rather than repeating what you have already said, your conclusion should help readers understand why your evaluation matters.
A strong conclusion shows that your criteria, analysis, and evidence come together to form a cohesive, thoughtful evaluation.
________________


I. Purpose of the Evaluation Conclusion
Your conclusion should accomplish the following goals:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              1. Restate your judgment in new words

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              2. Summarize how your three criteria support that judgment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              3. Explain the broader significance of your evaluation

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              4. Leave the reader with a final meaningful insight

This is your last opportunity to show the importance of your argument—make it thoughtful, clear, and reflective.
________________


II. What NOT to Do in the Conclusion
Avoid:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * repeating your thesis word-for-word,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * introducing new evidence,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * summarizing the plot again,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * giving your personal opinion,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * ending abruptly without final insight,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * using phrases like “In conclusion” or “In summary.”

Your writing should remain analytical, academic, and polished.
________________

1. Restated Judgment
Rephrase your thesis so it feels fresh and reflective.
2. Synthesis of Criteria
Show how your three criteria collectively support your evaluation.
3. Broader Significance
Explain what your evaluation reveals about:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * culture

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * storytelling

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * symbolic meaning

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * trauma

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * fear

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * morality

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * community identity

4. Final Insight
Leave the reader with a deeper or forward-looking idea.

Evaluation Essay Revision Guide
Revising your Evaluation Essay requires more than fixing grammar or formatting. Revision helps you strengthen your argument, clarify your reasoning, and demonstrate a deeper understanding of your subject. This guide walks you through a step-by-step process for improving your thesis, organization, evidence integration, analysis, and MLA formatting.
Use this guide after completing your draft and before submitting the final version.
________________


I. Revision Step 1: Revisit Your Thesis
A strong evaluation thesis must include:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        1. The subject

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        2. A clear judgment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        3. Three specific criteria

Thesis Revision Questions
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Does my thesis make a judgment, not just identify a topic?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Are my criteria specific and academic (symbolic depth, cultural relevance, narrative function, etc.)?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Can each criterion support a full paragraph?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Does my thesis avoid vague phrasing (e.g., “interesting,” “cool,” “scary”)?

Thesis Revision Checklist
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Subject identified

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Judgment clearly stated

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Three criteria listed

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * No conversational tone

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * No plot summary

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * No personal opinion language (“I think,” “I believe”)

If you cannot find all three criteria in your thesis, revise before moving on.

II. Revision Step 2: Evaluate Your Paragraph Structure
Each body paragraph should:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  3. Provide evidence

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 4. Include MLA in-text citations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 5. Analyze the evidence

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 6. Connect back to the thesis

Paragraph Revision Questions
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Does each paragraph begin with a clear criterion-based topic sentence?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Do I explain the criterion before giving evidence?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Do I use specific evidence instead of general statements?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Do I analyze the evidence (2–4 sentences per quote or scene)?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Do I connect the analysis to the criterion?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Do I connect the paragraph to the thesis at the end?

Paragraph-Level Checklist
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * One criterion per paragraph

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Topic sentence expresses a mini-judgment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Evidence is relevant and specific

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * MLA citation included

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Multiple sentences of analysis

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Criterion connection is explicit

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Conclusion sentence reinforces thesis

If your paragraph lacks analysis, add commentary explaining why the evidence matters.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * meaningful

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * supportive of your criteria

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Does my evidence directly support the criterion?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Have I avoided giving long plot summaries?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Are my citations correct for the type of source?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Folklore → (Article Author) or (“Source Title”)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Quote is followed by interpretation

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Paraphrases are also cited

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Do I explain why the evidence supports the criterion?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Do I interpret symbolism, cultural meaning, narrative function, or psychological themes?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Do I avoid repeating or summarizing the evidence?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Do I connect my analysis to the overall judgment?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Analysis directly connects to criterion

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Paragraph connects to thesis

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * No plot summary in the analysis

If your paragraph feels too descriptive, replace summary with interpretation.

Understanding Your Evaluation Essay Rubric: Student Guide
This guide explains the rubric in clear, student-friendly language so you know exactly what your instructor is looking for.

1. Thesis & Judgment
What this means:
 Your thesis must say what you are evaluating, what your judgment is, and the three criteria you will use.
Strong essays:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * State a clear judgment (“La Llorona is an effective cultural monster because…”).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Include all three criteria.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Avoid vague language like “interesting,” “cool,” or “scary.”

________________


2. Criteria Development
Each of your criteria should be:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * academic (symbolism, cultural relevance, emotional impact, etc.)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * clearly explained

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * used to structure a whole paragraph

Weak essays list criteria but do not explain or use them.
________________


3. Organization & Paragraph Structure
A strong essay:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * uses one criterion per paragraph

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * includes transitions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * ends paragraphs by connecting back to the thesis

Weak essays drift into plot summary or lose their structure.
________________


4. Use of Evidence
Evidence should be:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * specific

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * quoted or paraphrased

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * relevant

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * cited in MLA format

Avoid random details, long summary, or uncited paraphrases.
________________


5. Analysis & Interpretation
After every piece of evidence, you MUST explain:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * what it means

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * why it matters

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * how it proves your criterion

Summary is NOT analysis.
________________


6. MLA Format
You need:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * in-text citations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * a Works Cited page

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * correct MLA formatting

Every source in your essay must appear in your Works Cited.
________________


7. Introduction & Conclusion
Your introduction should:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * introduce your subject,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * set up purpose,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * end with your thesis.

Your conclusion should:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * restate your judgment differently,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * synthesize criteria,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * explain why your evaluation matters.

8. Grammar, Style, Tone
Your writing should be:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * clear

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * academic

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * edited for correctness

Avoid informal words, fragments, run-ons, and conversational tone.

Subject: La Llorona
Judgment: An effective cultural monster

Introduction (Sample)
Folkloric monsters often reveal the emotional and cultural anxieties of the communities that pass their stories from one generation to the next. La Llorona, one of the most enduring figures in Mexican and Tejano folklore, is known for wandering riversides in eternal grief for the children she lost. Evaluating La Llorona demonstrates how folklore functions as emotional memory and cultural storytelling. Through the criteria of symbolic depth, cultural relevance, and emotional impact, La Llorona emerges as a meaningful and enduring cultural monster.
________________


Body Paragraph (Criterion 1: Symbolic Depth)
La Llorona demonstrates strong symbolic depth through her association with water and generational mourning. Symbolic depth refers to the layers of meaning a figure holds beyond its literal narrative. In many accounts, La Llorona is described crying along riverbanks for the children she drowned (“La Llorona: The Weeping Woman”). This recurring image symbolizes the weight of unresolved grief passed down through families and communities. The river becomes a metaphor for emotional wounds that continue to flow, emphasizing the ongoing nature of sorrow. This symbolism supports the criterion of symbolic depth because La Llorona represents the emotional legacies that communities carry across generations.
________________


Conclusion (Sample)
La Llorona remains an effective and influential cultural monster because her story captures emotional truths that resonate across communities. Her symbolic depth, lasting cultural relevance, and powerful emotional impact allow the legend to serve as a vessel for collective memory and communal storytelling. Evaluating her significance reveals how folklore helps communities express experiences of loss, identity, and fear that cannot always be spoken directly.


Introduction to Critical Analysis
Critical analysis is a foundational academic skill that asks you to look beyond the surface of a text to examine how and why it creates meaning. Unlike evaluation—which focuses on judging a subject using criteria—critical analysis focuses on interpreting, explaining, and unpacking the choices authors, artists, and creators make.
In ENGL 1302, you will use critical analysis to examine literature, graphic novels, television episodes, narrative structures, monster representations, and cultural symbols. This section will guide you step-by-step through the skills required to analyze texts thoughtfully and academically.


What Is Critical Analysis?
Critical analysis is a reading and writing skill that goes beyond simply understanding what a text says. Instead, it focuses on how a text creates meaning through choices in structure, imagery, language, visuals, pacing, symbolism, and more.
Critical analysis asks you to become an investigator, not a spectator.
________________


I. Definition of Critical Analysis
Critical Analysis:
A method of interpreting a text by breaking it down into parts, examining how those parts function, and explaining how they contribute to the text’s deeper meaning, themes, or emotional impact.
Critical analysis always asks:
What is the creator doing, how are they doing it, and why does it matter?
You are not judging whether the text is “good” or “bad.”
You are explaining how meaning is constructed—and what effect it has.
________________

III. Examples of Critical Analysis vs. Summary
Students often confuse the two.
Here are clear examples:
SUMMARY (Incorrect for analysis):
“Will is trapped in the Upside Down, and his mom tries to communicate with him through the lights.”
CRITICAL ANALYSIS (Correct):
“The use of flickering lights as a communication method symbolizes the fragile and unreliable connection between worlds. The creators’ choice to use domestic, everyday objects highlights how supernatural events invade ordinary life.”
Why this works:
 It explains how the detail creates meaning.
________________


Another example:
SUMMARY:
 “At the beginning of the graphic novel, Eleven is in the lab and looks scared.”
ANALYSIS:
 “The close-up panel of Eleven’s eyes conveys fear and lack of control. By isolating her expression, the illustrator invites the reader to share her emotional vulnerability, setting a tone of confinement and surveillance.”
Why this works:
 It interprets the panel design and emotional effect.
________________


IV. The Three Core Questions of Critical Analysis
Every strong analytical paragraph answers these:
1. What did you notice?
(Details, patterns, symbols, techniques)
2. What does it mean?
(Interpretation, symbolism, themes)
3. Why does it matter?
(Cultural, emotional, psychological, narrative significance)
Example (using Stranger Things):
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               1. Notice: The lab is portrayed with sterile blues and greys.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               2. Meaning: These colors suggest coldness, control, and dehumanization.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               3. Why it matters: It reinforces the theme of children being treated as experiments rather than human beings.
