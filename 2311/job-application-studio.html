Job Application Studio

A practice room for the job application package: resume, cover letter, employer research, and LinkedIn brand work.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All majors

Purpose

Assemble a job application package: professional resume, tailored cover letter for a technical writing role, employer-expectations research, LinkedIn brand work, then place the package on your portfolio site.

On this page

Required reading / assignment overview

Titles and how to use them live here. Library: SAC Library · A–Z databases.

How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose above.
  2. Open the assignment overview dropdowns for structure and required chapters.
  3. Use Tools for purpose statements, outlines, source notes, and peer rehearsal.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).
Assignment overview
  • Part 1: comprehensive professional resume emphasizing technical writing skills and relevant experience.
  • Part 2: tailored cover letter; revise after peer or acquaintance feedback.
  • Part 3: research employer expectations (interviews, LinkedIn, employer sites, or HR input).
  • Part 4: LinkedIn profile and networking plan; compile the packet on your portfolio or site.
Required reading (course text)
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin’s, ISBN 9781319567408.
  • Markel chapter: Ch. 15 Applying for a Job. Canvas does not state a citation style for the job packet itself.
