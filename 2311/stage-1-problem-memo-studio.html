Stage overview
  • Due: Sun Jan 31
  • What you make: A 1 to 2 page memo to Professor Davis naming the problem, the decision maker, the stakeholders affected, and your locked question; the completed Audience Profile Sheet; and an AI use statement.
  • Length: 1 to 2 page memo plus the Audience Profile Sheet. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: APA 7.
  • Course outcomes: 1, 2, 3
  • Support built in: Start Here path in Canvas; Week 2 check; early help if a stage is missing.
  • Question Lock shape: "How can [named organization or team] solve [named problem] for [named reader who makes the decision], and why is that the best option?" Keep confidential employer data and anyone's private information out of your documents.
Required reading for this stage
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin's, ebook through AlamoBOOKS+ in Canvas.
  • Ch. 1 Introduction to Technical Communication
  • Ch. 2 Ethical and Legal Considerations
  • Ch. 5 Analyzing Your Audience and Purpose
  • Ch. 14 Corresponding in Print and Online
  • APA 7 help: Purdue OWL APA
How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 1: Problem Memo and Audience Profile when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
