Project 1 · Audience Analysis

Audience Analysis Studio

A practice room for the Audience Analysis report and presentation. Characterize readers, then plan how the document and visuals will meet them.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All majors

Purpose

Characterize a specific audience for a technical document (demographics, expertise, purpose, attitudes, design preferences), then present the analysis. Draft a 5 to 7 page Audience Profile Report in APA and a 10-minute podcast or video presentation with slides or a handout.

How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose above.
  2. Open the assignment overview dropdowns for structure and required chapters.
  3. Use Tools for purpose statements, outlines, source notes, and peer rehearsal.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).
Assignment overview
  • Choose a specific audience for a hypothetical or real technical document (may feed later proposal/report work).
  • Cover selection; characterization (use an Audience Profile sheet); purpose of engagement; attitudes and expectations; graphics and design; methods for learning about the audience (interview, reading, search); social media insights; cultural variables.
  • Deliverables: Audience Profile Report (5 to 7 pages, APA, easy to navigate) plus a 10-minute presentation (podcast or video) with PowerPoint, Canva, or handout.
  • Respect privacy and ethics when gathering audience data.
Required reading (course text)
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin’s, ISBN 9781319567408 (AlamoBOOKS+ / Canvas Textbook Material).
  • Primary chapter for this project: Ch. 5 Analyzing Audience and Purpose.
  • Supporting chapters in the early sequence: Ch. 1 Introduction to Technical Communication; Ch. 2 Ethical and Legal Considerations; Ch. 10 Correct and Effective Sentences; Ch. 14 Corresponding in Print and Online; plus APA formatting guidance used in class.
  • Course Map docs that mention Open Oregon Press chapter sequences are outdated; use Markel 14th edition chapter numbers from modules.
