Workplace Problem Project

The seven stages

Stage 1 · due Sun Jan 31Problem Memo and Audience ProfileA 1 to 2 page memo to Professor Davis naming the problem, the decision maker, the stakeholders affected, and your locked question; the completed Audience Profile Sheet; and an AI use statement. Stage 2 · due Sun Feb 14Research ProposalA proposal to your decision maker: the problem, your proposed research (secondary sources plus one primary method), a task schedule, and how the results will be judged. Use the Canvas "How to Write Your Proposal" guide and sample. Stage 3 · due Thu Feb 25Annotated Sources and Research NotesFive sources: at least three credible secondary sources and one primary source (a short interview, questionnaire, or observation). Annotate each one for what it means for your reader's decision. Stage 4 · due Sun Mar 7Options and Criteria MemoTwo or three options, the shared criteria (one must be an ethics or stakeholder criterion), and which source supports each judgment. The criteria table is your first graphic. Stage 5 · due Thu Mar 25Recommendation Report DraftProblem, method, options, criteria, analysis, recommendation, references in APA, and at least two purposeful graphics. Stage 6 · due Sun Apr 11Usability Test Report and Revision MemoTwo or three readers (classmates acting as your audience, plus one real reader if possible) try three short tasks with your draft, such as "find the cost" or "find the recommendation." Log what they could and could not do, then write a memo naming each planned change and an editing pass. Stage 7 · due Thu Apr 22Final PacketYour revised Recommendation Report (APA), a Process Record with three required sentences and an AI use statement, and a checklist pointing to evidence for all seven outcomes. A packet missing evidence for any outcome does not pass yet.

Late April to May 14

After the Final Packet

  • Packet feedback: Professor Davis returns Final Packet feedback by Thu Apr 29.
  • Recommendation Briefing: A 3 to 5 minute briefing to your decision maker, live or recorded, with three to five slides (Markel and Selber Ch. 21). It is built on your finished report.
  • Professional Launch practice: A resume and a tailored cover letter for a real posting in the field connected to your problem, with a peer review (Ch. 15). This is practice. LinkedIn work is optional.
  • Digital Portfolio (optional): A portfolio site is optional. Your Process Record already does the reflection.
  • Revision window: If your Final Packet is missing outcome evidence, a revision window closes Sun May 9. Check Canvas for how it works in your section.
  • Final exam period: Briefings are shared during the final exam period if the section meets then.
