Recommendation Report Studio

A practice room for the recommendation report. Evaluate options against clear criteria and recommend one course of action.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All majors

Purpose

Build on your proposal into a recommendation report: identify the problem or opportunity, evaluate options against clear criteria, and recommend a course of action with research depth and clarity. Aim for a maximum of 1,000 words. Submit as PDF, Google Doc, or Word in MLA.

How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose above.
  2. Open the assignment overview dropdowns for structure and required chapters.
  3. Use Tools for purpose statements, outlines, source notes, and peer rehearsal.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).
Assignment overview
  • Expand the Proposal (and progress work) into a formal recommendation report.
  • Structure: Introduction (problem + criteria); Methods (options); Results (evaluate each option against the same criteria); Conclusions; Recommendations.
  • Keep research deep and objective; show how findings grew since the proposal.
  • Length: Maximum 1,000 words.
  • Citation: MLA formatting for this major (Majors 1 and 2 use APA). Submit PDF, Google Doc, or Word in MLA.
Required reading (course text)
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin’s, ISBN 9781319567408.
  • Markel chapters for this major: Ch. 18 Writing Recommendation Reports; Ch. 11 Designing Print and Digital Documents; Ch. 12 Creating Graphics; Ch. 13 Evaluating and Testing Documents (usability work in the same module).
