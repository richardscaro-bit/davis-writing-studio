Digital Portfolio Studio

A practice room for the final digital portfolio and progress report. Curate work, design the site, and reflect on your development.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All majors

Purpose

Curate a digital portfolio website of your technical writing from the course, include the job application packet, and write an 800 to 1,000 word progress report on your development (APA or consistent personal brand formatting).

On this page

Required reading / assignment overview

Titles and how to use them live here. Library: SAC Library · A–Z databases.

How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose above.
  2. Open the assignment overview dropdowns for structure and required chapters.
  3. Use Tools for purpose statements, outlines, source notes, and peer rehearsal.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).
Assignment overview
  • Curate pieces that show skill, range, and progression; include the Job Application Packet.
  • Progress report (800 to 1,000 words): initial skills; challenges; key learnings; feedback impact; future aspirations.
  • Site sections: bio; curated documents; progress report; job packet; contact. Keep navigation clean; test links; keep the site viewable.
Required reading / materials
  • Your own course documents (the portfolio content).
  • Platform help as needed (Canva site builder, Wix, Squarespace, Weebly, or another clean template).
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin’s, ISBN 9781319567408.
  • Markel chapters already used across the term remain your craft reference. Progress report: APA or your own personal brand formatting (800 to 1,000 words).
