Stage overview
  • Due: Thu Apr 22
  • What you make: Your revised Recommendation Report (APA), a Process Record with three required sentences and an AI use statement, and a checklist pointing to evidence for all seven outcomes. A packet missing evidence for any outcome does not pass yet.
  • Length: Revised report plus Process Record. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: APA 7.
  • Course outcomes: All seven
  • Support built in: Final Packet help sessions; Writing Center hours.
  • Process Record sentences: (1) your locked question, your reader, and how your recommendation answers it; (2) the source you relied on for a criterion and the source you set aside; (3) what a test reader could not do, what you changed, and what the reader can do now.
Required reading for this stage
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin's, ebook through AlamoBOOKS+ in Canvas.
  • Ch. 17 Writing Informational Reports (review as needed)
  • Ch. 18 Writing Recommendation Reports (review as needed)
  • APA 7 help: Purdue OWL APA
How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 7: Final Packet when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
