Stage overview
  • Due: Sun Feb 14
  • What you make: A proposal to your decision maker: the problem, your proposed research (secondary sources plus one primary method), a task schedule, and how the results will be judged. Use the Canvas "How to Write Your Proposal" guide and sample.
  • Length: 3 to 4 pages. Check Canvas for the final requirement.
  • Citation: APA 7.
  • Course outcomes: 1, 2, 4
  • Support built in: Week 4 conference; pair check; one allowed revision of your question; library session.

How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose and the stage overview.
  2. Open Tools and pick Stage 2: Research Proposal when a tool asks which stage you are on.
  3. Bring a question or a passage of your own writing to the Tutor. It asks questions; it does not write for you or grade.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition) and state any AI or tutor use.
