Research Proposal Studio

A practice room for the research proposal. Define the problem, outline the proposed program, and gather supporting research.

Practice path Practice tools Resources All majors

Purpose

Draft a 5 to 7 page APA research proposal for a problem or need in your field: purpose, summary, introduction, proposed program, qualifications, budget, appendixes, task schedule, evaluation techniques, and references.

How to use this studio
  1. Read the Purpose above.
  2. Open the assignment overview dropdowns for structure and required chapters.
  3. Use Tools for purpose statements, outlines, source notes, and peer rehearsal.
  4. Keep AI at Level 2 (support, not composition).
Assignment overview
  • Conceptualize, research, and draft a professional proposal for internal and external stakeholders.
  • Structure: Purpose; Summary; Introduction; Proposed Program; Qualifications and Experience; Budget; Appendixes; Task Schedule; Description of Evaluation Techniques; References.
  • Length: 5 to 7 pages with APA references. Define deliverables and metrics; keep tone professional; keep the solution original to the need.
Required reading (course text)
  • Textbook: Mike Markel and Stuart A. Selber, Technical Communication, 14th edition (2025), Bedford/St. Martin’s, ISBN 9781319567408.
  • Markel chapters: Ch. 6 Researching Your Subject; Ch. 7 Organizing Your Information; Ch. 16 Writing Proposals.
  • Annotated bibliography draft work in the module supports this proposal.
Practice helpers

Pick Research Proposal inside Tools when asked. Citation practice: transfer MLA-tool structure to APA for this project.
