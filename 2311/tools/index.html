Writing tools

Helpers for whichever stage you are on. Open a tool, choose your stage if that helper asks, then draft a purpose statement, outline, citation practice, source notes, peer notes, or keywords.

Purpose

Tools stay generic until you pick a stage. Every 2311 stage uses APA 7. The citation helper practices MLA form, so carry the structure over to APA 7 as you go.
